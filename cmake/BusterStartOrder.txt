# function of one of main.c's units, placed by its unit's object and its section, and `- NAME` for one of the C
# library's, placed by its name; the parallel split puts a unit's functions of .text.buster_checker first in that
# section in this order (cmake/BusterStartOrder.cmake, docs/commands/build.md, "The start's code order").

# Written by `compiler start-order` from main at 2d8174328 with #2873's asks of the include search, built on the 9700X
# as the benchmark builds are (BUSTER_HOST_ARCH=znver5 BUSTER_DEBUG_INFO=0, Release, CI's toolchain: Clang 23.1.4 and
# its lld): its `readelf -sW`, and `compiler start-trace` of each command, which lists the functions it runs exactly:
# an empty file's -fsyntax-only and -E -P, tiny.c's -c and hello.c's -c (the inputs of buster-performance's
# docs/measurements/checker-start-order-1444-2026-11-14), the -c of each of the frozen corpus's 1,336 units of musl,
# a process a unit (the selection and options of buster-performance's
# docs/measurements/kernel-census-musl-2873-2026-11-16), and sqlite3.c's -c, in that order: each command's functions
# that the ones before it didn't run, in address order, and musl's by how many of its units ran each, most first.
