    bench: enter the broker through the same-PID gate and reconcile every activation
    
    Implements the #1162 criterion selected in comment 5858483525:
    
    - the broker unit's only ExecStart is the static entry gate, which
      fexecve()s the installed broker in the same PID after its checks;
    - issue1162_broker_entry_evidence.py requires, for every activated
      instance, one exact PID1 start/terminal, observer lifecycle and MainPID,
      gate PASS (same PID, stream, start ticks, FD0, preceding the broker,
      installed tuples/hashes/accounts equal to a before/after root readback)
      and complete allowlisted broker diagnostic with a matching outcome;
    - the slice installs the gate, takes the outside readbacks and uses the new
      consumer for the broker verdict; the earlier every-broker external /proc
      consumer still runs and is retained, and every complete external capture
      must agree with the joined identity;
    - observer and old consumer treat the gate as a known pre-exec image.
    
    Replaying the consumer on Attempt 23's real journal/observer data with a
    synthetic PASS per instance joins all 11 activations (including the 1-3 ms
    @5-@7); the real Attempt 23 journal has no PASS and stays failed.
