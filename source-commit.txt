    bench: finish signal cancellations and their recovery (#880 attempt P)
    
    Attempt P sent one SIGTERM to the service after the prepare manifest. The
    cancel path appended CANCEL and ran bq_worker_stop while the cancel flag
    was still set, so bq_systemd_join returned BQ_WORKER_CANCEL_SIGNAL without
    reaping the start-outer launcher and bq_worker_stop reported
    cleanup-failed. The failure path published cancelled evidence and left
    the job active.
    
    Recovery then finished the job as interrupted. The queue only accepts a
    cancelled outcome for a job with a durable CANCEL, and the published
    outcome record already said cancelled, so every retry failed without a
    journal change while holding the lease.
    
    - join takes a cancellable flag. The main wait stays cancellable; the
      cleanup reap runs only after the outer unit is proven empty and is not.
    - Recovery without a durable outcome adopts an existing outcome record by
      exact bytes, limited to outcomes the queue accepts; otherwise it uses
      cancelled for a durable cancellation and interrupted for the rest.
      Other existing bytes fail closed and are never replaced.
    - Regression tests: the real systemd join under a pending cancellation,
      and cancelled-job recovery (converges; fails closed on foreign bytes).
      Each fails on the old behaviour.
