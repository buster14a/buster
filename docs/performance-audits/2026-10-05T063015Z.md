## Correction and retained evidence

This addendum corrects the scope of the section-identity conclusion in [2026-10-04T104111Z](2026-10-04T104111Z.md), originally published by #2606 at `bf29b533c49a81e220c41d5a37ca91c3f24230dd`. The original audit, scripts and all 35 archived evidence files remain unchanged. The attribution/research question remains with [#1028](https://github.com/buster14a/buster/issues/1028) / [#2607](https://github.com/buster14a/buster/issues/2607). No production change or new compiler/performance result is claimed.

The [archived identity script](evidence/2026-10-04T104111Z/identity_probe.sh) uses `objcopy -O binary --only-section=<name>` to compare ELF section contents. That binary-output mode emits loadable section contents; it is unsuitable as a general identity extractor for non-ALLOC debug/symbol sections. The archived [section hashes](evidence/2026-10-04T104111Z/sections.sha256) assign SHA-256 of empty content and length zero to `.debug_info`, `.debug_abbrev`, `.debug_line`, `.debug_str`, `.symtab` and `.strtab`. The [verdict](evidence/2026-10-04T104111Z/verdict.txt) therefore labels those extracted empty outputs as same. It has not compared their real payloads. The preserved `readelf -S -W` records show those sections exist and are nonempty.

Retain these conclusions supported by the original receipts:

- The `-g0` self-compile whole-file hashes match, as do the SQLite `-g0` output hashes.
- The extracted loadable `.text`, `.rodata`, `.data` and `.eh_frame` section hashes match under `-g`, supporting the recorded generated-code identity.
- `readelf` sizes show `.debug_info` shrinks by 429,675 bytes and `.debug_str` by 55 bytes in the recorded `-g` comparison.
- The work-ledger, callgrind instruction counts and hosted diagnostic timings are separate observations and are unaffected by this extraction limitation.

**Withdraw the stronger claim that these are the only content differences under `-g`.** Equal sizes of the other debug sections establish equal sizes only. Their byte contents, symbol tables, complete debug semantics and whether the removed DIEs are required remain unverified. Whole-file `-g` hashes differ. Publishing this addendum does not validate the diagnostic-dropping probe as a production candidate or discharge dedicated-host admission.

## Focused tool control and next action

A new local Linux tool control on 2026-10-05 uses a C fixture `int main(void) { return 0; }`, compiled with Clang `-g`, and GNU objcopy 2.47. The old `-O binary --only-section=.debug_info` method produces 0 bytes; `objcopy --dump-section .debug_info=<output> <fixture>` produces 55 bytes. This control reproduces the extractor distinction only; it does not recreate any original Buster binary or timing. The original binaries were removed by the archived script and have not been supplied to this review.

Any future evidence replay should extract every intended real section with `--dump-section` or another verified section reader, require the extracted length to equal its ELF header size for file-backed sections, and distinguish absent/NOBITS from extraction failure. Include `.debug_loc` and `.debug_ranges`, omitted by the original comparison loop. Preserve every replayed source/tree/compiler/output identity. Until then, report unverified non-ALLOC payload equality as unknown.
