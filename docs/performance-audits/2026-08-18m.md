This is the boundary lesson in concrete form: the profitable unit was not a
new AVX-512 instruction but a 277k-member homogeneous population that had
lost the context needed to reuse its prepared bytes.  One compact authority
now serves normal rows, expansion chunks, and allocator edits.  Future SIMD
kernels should be sought after identifying the next repeated consumer of an
existing lane; do not build a second sparse stream or batch isolated rows just
to expose vector syntax.
