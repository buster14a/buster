The 75,214 parameter stores come from 58,419 parameter edges (48,677 with one
copy). The IR side is already clean: 355 are self-copies, none sit on a
single-predecessor block, and no parameter receives one value on every edge.

parameterized block started from memory. Among in-block sites, 27,800 spills evict the owner of a
fixed physical destination; many owners were dying values staged into an
