## Workload

Every arm compiles the same input, the base revision's unity self-host source,
from the base checkout:

```sh
ide cc -Isrc -Ibuild/generated -DBUSTER_UNITY_BUILD=1 -DBUSTER_INCLUDE_TESTS=0 \
  -target x86_64-unknown-linux -march=znver5 -g0 -c \
  [-fc-ast-pilot] src/buster/apps/ide/ide.c
```

There are four arms, counted with Callgrind (`--cache-sim=no --branch-sim=no`):
- A: base, default flags;
- B: base with `-fc-ast-pilot`;
- C: candidate with `-fc-ast-pilot`;
- D: candidate, default flags.

## Census (base, B arm)

A throwaway build of the base, not committed, wrapped the classification's
pieces in noinline functions and counted categories. Its `-g0` object is
byte-identical to B's.
- **Statements:** `c_ir_lower_body_advance` classifies 155,771 statement first
  tokens. 85,958 are identifiers bound to an entity, 65,611 are unbound
  identifiers and 4,202 are not identifiers.

| Piece | Ir | Calls |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Typedef probe: entity, then for an unbound identifier the scope lookup and `c_parse_type_start_token` | 38.54 M | 155,771 (65,611 take the lookup) |
| `c_parse_type_word_for_dialect` (two sites) | 9.00 M | 102,661 |
| Label proof (`c_ir_named_label_at` and `c_parse_label_candidate_at`) | 5.75 M | 155,771, of which 12 are labels |
| `c_ir_control_substatement_position` from the switch label scan | 0.22 M | 5,224, of which 0 answer true |

- **Unbound identifiers:** 53,835 of the 65,611 are statement keywords from
  `C_IR_STATEMENT_KEYWORD_SET`, such as `if`, `return` and `for`. 77 of those
  are `asm` or `static_assert`. The remaining 11,776 answer "typedef" 11,731
  times, 6,826 of them as type words.
- **Why the keyword lookups are wasted:** a keyword is never a type word and
  never an entity's name, so the probe always answers false for one.
- **What the gate saved:** a throwaway prototype of the gate, measured before
  the budgets were written, cut A and B by 24.93 M each, with identical
  objects. It is not the measured candidate.

The 77 extra lookups are the unbound `asm` and `static_assert` first tokens.
The census counted its keywords with the wider `C_IR_STATEMENT_KEYWORD_SET`,
and the gate deliberately leaves both words out: some dialects spell ordinary
identifiers that way, so gating them would not be exact.

A second throwaway census run counted those first tokens directly and found
exactly 77, so 11,776 + 77 = 11,853. The ceiling was written from the wrong
set. The gate behaves as designed.

## The tree reads (negative)

- **`c_ir_control_substatement_position` from the switch label scan.** Its
  whole cost is 0.22 M over 5,224 calls, and none answers true. It is below
  the 1 M bar before any table is charged, so nothing is built.
- **`c_ir_lower_body_advance`'s first-token classification.**
  - **Ceiling.** Net of the gate, what a tree could replace is at most the
    rest of the typedef probe (about 15 M, most of it the entity checks
    every statement makes), the type-word test (9.0 M) and the label proof
    (5.7 M). That is under 30 M.
  - **Table cost.** Answering from the tree needs a per-body table keyed by
    first token over every classified statement: 155,771 entries, against
    the extent table's 39,721. #3495 measured whole-body tables at about 400
    to 700 Ir per entry. An expression statement's first token also costs a
    scan of its subtree for the least anchor (`c_ir_statement_extents_first`).
  - **Verdict.** Even the low end, about 62 M, is twice the ceiling. Nothing
    is built. A cheaper read would need a token-to-node map that the tree
    does not have. That is the same follow-up #3495 left open.

## Validation and limits

- Callgrind counts instructions, not time, on `-march=x86-64-v3` builds whose
  AVX-512 kernels are compiled out.
- One input, one host, single process, no pinning and no `perf` counters. Wall
  time was not measured: the budgets did not ask for it, and on this host it
  cannot resolve an effect of this size.
- **Census builds:** both are throwaway and uncommitted. Their counts come
  from instrumented copies of the base, and their objects match B's.
- **Fixture:** `c_test_statement_keyword_typedef_probes` pins the gate. A
  keyword-led body makes no probe, a labelled body makes exactly one, and a
  typedef-led declaration still lowers.
- **Acceptance:** no 9700X request is made. Zen 5 validation stays
  **incomplete** (#2761).
