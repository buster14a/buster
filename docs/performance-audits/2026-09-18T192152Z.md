on the same VM, with separate clean checkouts/builds and AB/BA/AB order across
three trials. It preserves the complete native-driver-owned matrix and records
source SHA separately from workflow SHA. Independent coverage validation and
matching driver assertion inventories are required. The workflow documentation
is [windows-matrix-benchmark.md](../windows-matrix-benchmark.md).

The initial pair pins baseline `f7c4e868` and guarded candidate `b03bdf76`.
CPU consumption and peak simultaneous process-tree RSS are unavailable; wall
durations are not substitutes for them. Whole-job duration, queue delay and
aggregate runner seconds must be reported separately from matrix duration.
