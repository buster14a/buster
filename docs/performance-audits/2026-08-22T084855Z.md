misses: one per-value prepass, one per-field-access memset, and the copy sites
that own a fifth of them**).  The cache-miss counterpart to the 2026-08-21
branch-miss survey, and the same shape of document: a budget, a ranked list,
and the leads the next audits are expected to take.  **No source change**;
this entry is the measurement, plus `tools/cache_miss_survey.py`, which is the
whole method as a script so the next reading is a command rather than an
afternoon.

Two things follow immediately.  **The TLBs and the fault path are not levers**
— 72 K page walks and 2.014 faults across ten billion instructions — so the
arenas' large committed chunks are already doing their job, and the `CToken`
48->16 result's fault win (`2026-08-08k` line of work) has no successor here.
And **the miss budget is a latency budget, not a count budget**: nine tenths
of the 124 M L1d misses are answered by L2 at about fourteen cycles, while the
7,26 M fills that reach DRAM cost 250 or more.  At 250 cycles a fill those
alone are 1,81 G cycles against a 3,95 G-cycle run — 46% — which is an upper
bound, since the machine overlaps and prefetches most of it, but it is the
reason this survey ranks by DRAM fills and treats L1d as the secondary column.

**Method.**  `tools/cache_miss_survey.py`, four captures over the same
workload: `cycles` at 1/100003 as the denominator, `L1-dcache-load-misses` at
1/2003, `l2_cache_req_stat.ls_rd_blk_c` at 1/211 and
`ls_any_fills_from_sys.dram_io_all` at 1/251, each aggregated by leaf symbol
and printed beside its cycles share, so the column that matters is the ratio:
a symbol at 1,0 misses in proportion to the time it spends, and one at 3,0 is
waiting on memory.  Top symbols are broken down by source line through
`llvm-symbolizer` on symbol value plus perf's offset — never `-F srcline`, for
the reason `AGENTS.md` gives — with the inline chain kept, so a line inside an
inlined callee is reported under the callee's name.

Three properties of the measurement, each worth knowing before reading a
number out of it:

- **The events are not precise.**  AMD's precise facility is IBS, which
  samples system-wide only and so needs root; the script stays unprivileged,
  so every sample carries skid.  Symbols survive that, lines mostly do: read a
  line as its loop, not as its instruction.  `--ibs` prints the privileged
  recipe for when the question becomes *which object* misses rather than which
  code.
- **Frame-pointer unwinding skips the caller of a `memcpy`.**  `memcpy` and
  `memset` never push `rbp`, so the frame after the leaf is the return address
  of the caller's *caller* — the fp tables name the site one level out, which
  is a real trap when a fifth of all samples land in libc.  `--dwarf` takes a
  second, sparser capture that unwinds with DWARF and names the exact call;
  the two disagree exactly as predicted (fp said "`c_gen.c:7640`", the call to
  `c_ir_emit_field_place_from_value`; DWARF said `c_gen.c:3682`, the `memset`
  inside it).
- **Shares are stable in L1d and loose in DRAM.**  Two full runs an hour apart
  agreed to 0,15 percentage points on every L1d share and to about 2 points on
  the DRAM ones (the top symbol read 21,26% and 23,58%), and the L1d miss
  total itself moved 123,99 M to 128,64 M, ~4%.  Quote DRAM shares as "about a
  fifth", not as three digits.

**Hotspot 1: the x86 selector's per-value prepass — 23,58% of DRAM fills,
13,18% of L1d misses, 3,7x its cycles share.**
`machine_select_canonical_function_x86_64` tops every miss event, and it is
the only symbol whose line breakdown is a single loop:
`machine_x86_64.c:4248`-`4258` holds its six hottest lines and, together,
**14,17% of every DRAM fill in the compile**.  The loop runs once per value of
every function and does two things per iteration.  It writes four *separately
arena-allocated* parallel arrays (`promotable_locals` as `u8`, then
`value_last_use_ordinals`, `local_store_counts` and `value_def_ordinals` as
`u32`) — four cold streams where the layout admits one; those three `u32`
stores alone are 4,80% of DRAM fills.  Then it reads
`function->instructions[definition.value]` at a random index (6,17%, the
single hottest line in the survey) to test two fields, `opcode` and `result`.
`IrInstruction` is 64 bytes: that is a whole line fetched for 8 useful bytes,
12% useful information per line, about a million times per compile.  `IrValue`
is already the compacted 16-byte record `2026-08-08k` made it, and the
sequential read of it at `:4254` is only 1,57% — the streaming access is not
the problem, the random one is.  The shape of the fix is the one `2026-08-22e`
already built for this file's other prepasses: an opcode/result projection or
a `MachineSelectionRowLayout`-style row walk, plus one interleaved row for the
four output arrays.  This is the largest single lever the survey found.

**Hotspot 2: a per-field-access `memset` of a types-count array — 6,74% of all
L1d misses in one line.**  `c_ir_emit_field_place_from_value` at
`c_gen.c:3682` zeroes a `visited` array sized `program->types.count + 1` on
**every field access lowered**.  Instrumented (probe counters, dumped after
`compiler_driver_execute_c_single`, per the harness `2026-08-22f` used):
**72.167 calls, capacities summing to 1.257.994.291, peak 21.357** — a mean of
17.432 entries, so the compile `memset`s **1,26 GB** to serve searches whose
work list is a handful of nodes.  It also bump-allocates four more
`capacity`-sized arrays per call (17 bytes per type per call, ~296 KB a call)
that the search only sparsely touches.  The traffic stays in L2 — 6,74% of L1d
misses against 0,22% of DRAM fills — because the temporal arena hands back the
same addresses every call, which is why this costs bandwidth rather than
latency and why it never showed in a cycles profile.
`c_ir_promoted_member_path` (`c_gen.c:14044`, 0,65%) is the same code shape
and should be fixed with it.  Both want the same treatment: a
generation-stamped `u32` array zeroed once per compile, or an explicit visited
list, since the population actually visited is tiny.

**Hotspot 3: the copy sites — 27,85% of DRAM fills and 16,54% of L1d misses
have their leaf in libc.**  `__memcpy_avx512_unaligned_erms` is 14,60% of DRAM
fills, `__memset_avx512_unaligned_erms` 5,93%, `__memcmp_evex_movbe` 0,85%
(that last one is `string_equal`'s tail, `string.c:237`, and the callers
`2026-08-21d` converted to interned ids are already gone from the list).
DWARF-attributed, the copies rank:

    5,50%  c_preprocess @ c_source.c:6391          token ranges -> one array
    3,60%  link_objects @ link.c:717               section data concatenation
    3,06%  link_native_executable_... @ link.c:2125 section data into the image
    3,03%  link_elf_section_table_append @ link.c:1618  whole image, to grow it
    1,76%  machine_stream_flatten @ machine.c:1172 chunk list -> flat array
    1,41%  c_source_map_append @ c_source.c:229
    1,28%  object_from_canonical_codegen_module @ object.c:8583

These are the tree's "memcpy-per-append" family and they are not all equal.
`link.c:1618` copies the *entire* 35 MB executable into a larger arena block
purely to append section headers, which a reserved tail would remove outright.
`c_source.c:6391` materializes 1,38 M tokens by walking the produced ranges
and copying each — worth a single-range fast path at least, and the lexer
writing into the final array at best.  `machine_stream_flatten` is the
chunked-stream builder paying for its own flattening once per function.  The
linker's two section-data copies are closer to compulsory: the bytes have to
reach the image once.  Ranked by what is removable rather than by size,
`link.c:1618` first.

**What the survey ruled out.**  Page faults (2.014) and both TLBs are noise.
L1i misses are 4,4 M, 3,6% of the data-side figure, so instruction footprint
is not the story despite the unity build's 40 KB functions.
`arena_allocate_bytes` carries 4,13% of L1d misses at 0,48x its cycles share —
it is touched by everything, and it is *under*-missing for its weight; there
is no lever in the allocator itself.  Likewise `c_ir_lower_frame_fallback` is
the second-largest L1d entry (9,25%) at 0,22x the cycles share: a large
function that misses less than its time would predict.  Neither belongs on a
list of cache leads.
