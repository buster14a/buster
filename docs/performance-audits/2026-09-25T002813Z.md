## Decision

**No-go for the minimal frozen-prefix function-body parallel prototype in
#531.** The serial trace confirms that a zero-append body can consume a
canonical type published by an earlier body. It also finds shared lazy-memo
fills in the SQLite input that the append-based eligibility test does not
exclude. Neither serial zero-append counts nor a type-read exclusion alone
establishes an immutable snapshot or a safe private builder. The proposed
kernel would still need a complete frozen-read and deferred-publication proof,
stable canonical IDs, ordered diagnostics, and a measured preparation/merge
budget. No production parallel lowerer was implemented.

This is a bounded decision about the currently proposed cut, not evidence
that single-TU parallelism is impossible. #531 remains the owner if a future
design can discharge those obligations. The earlier [source-only ownership
recheck](https://github.com/buster14a/buster/blob/50d603af42afeb8ebc466459b7fe2587f2f1deee/docs/performance-audits/2026-09-24T123141Z.md)
predicted the producer/consumer problem; this
hosted census observes its frequency on seven pinned inputs. The preliminary
[one-line audit entry](2026-09-24T125648Z.md) is retained unchanged; this is
the completed addendum.

The self-compile has 5,144 traced body rows: 5,142 successful and two
`skip-unneeded`. Its 1,088 publishers account for 1,191,904 of 1,906,817

such types in total. This is a direct witness against treating all 4,054
zero-delta bodies as independent.

SQLite has 39 successful bodies that read 102 earlier-body types; 14 of them
also have zero append deltas. Four bodies fill an ABI memo lazily; **two are
still in the provisional 2,108-body frozen-prefix population**. There are
433 canonical type appends outside the hooked publication sites. The observed
candidate count is therefore deliberately labeled provisional, not an
admission set. The 361-function C fixture and the synthetic extreme inputs
show that a body population can have no observed publications, but they do not
erase dependencies on the real self-compile and SQLite inputs.

## Capacity and cost interpretation

For the self-compile, 5,134 matched bodies total **335.0 ms of historical
serial body time**. Publishers account for 204.64 ms (61.1%); the provisional
frozen-prefix set accounts for 130.33 ms (38.9%). The analyzer's idealized
source-order take-index model, excluding every preparation, synchronization,
private-fragment, abort, replay, and merge cost, gives at eight lanes:

| Modeled scope | Eight-lane body makespan | What the number omits |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| All bodies with private views | 59.6 ms | A correct private/ordered shared-state publication design is not established. |
| Provisional frozen-prefix bodies, then publishers serially | 16.3 + 204.6 = 220.9 ms | Deferred-body validity, abort/replay, and all overhead are unproven. |

The longest joined body alone took 29.97 ms in the historical trace. A
provisional 114 ms idealized reduction in **body time** cannot be converted
into a whole-compiler speedup: the timing trace is from an older revision;
eight bodies are unmatched; the serial/publisher fraction dominates; and
body-private IR, scratch, source/debug cursors, error ordering, and ordered
fragment merge all have costs. An aborted attempt adds work before a serial
replay. The few-large synthetic population exercises a separate imbalance
limit; the many-small one exercises per-item overhead. Neither had a timed
parallel implementation to compare.

## Follow-up boundary

Keep the current serial lowerer. Before another prototype, enumerate every
read and write reached from `c_ir_lower_body` and `c_ir_ssa_finish`, including
the observed canonical append/lookup sites and SQLite ABI memo; show which
facts are fully frozen after preparation, which can be private, and how stable
IDs and earliest diagnostics merge in source order. A smaller independently
valuable slice may be evaluated separately if that proof is credible. Only
then use `lane_run` with one-lane equivalence and a bounded 1/2/4/8-worker
experiment; compare unchanged outputs, full compiler wall time, CPU work,
peak memory, preparation/replay/merge overhead, and same-source build controls
on the authorized measurement route. No 9700X measurement, race test,
sanitizer comparison, parallel prototype, or throughput gain is claimed by
this census. It makes no change to #36 or its acceptance policy.
