## Ordinary selection storage census

Temporary serial counters cover all 14 selector streams: the original ten
plus varargs and inline-assembly descriptor/operand/relocation streams. The
manifest maps stream IDs. They count actual chunk allocation requests, row
payload, flatten copies, same-arena borrowing, call/slot column-projection
writes, chunk-index allocations/copies and actual lookup calls. The wrapper
resets the arena high-water field for each selection, then restores its prior
maximum. Copy bytes below include output projection stores; they are not a
memory-bandwidth measurement. Alignment is captured in actual high water;
stream reservation itself counts chunk headers plus row capacity.

Each cell is one ordinary FAST native compile/link, not warm replay. Totals
sum arena suballocations over functions, and **are not simultaneously live
memory, malloc counts, touched pages or RSS**. High water is the maximum one
function's selector arena span, including transient allocation. Encoder
auxiliary streams and downstream edge-splitting copies are outside this
selector census; their execution remains included in native measurements.
