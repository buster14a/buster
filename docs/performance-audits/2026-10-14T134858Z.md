## Decision and scope

Retain a bounded expected-work implementation for review under #1362. The
arena-owned hash index replaces the all-prior-type-record scan in
`llvm_bc_add_type_record`, preserves exact `(code, operand count, operand
words)` equality and first-seen wire IDs, and caches each record's hash for
index growth. Forced full-hash collisions still compare the complete keys.
The output table remains ordered. This is an alternative to the issue's
crit-bit model; it does not provide that model's collision-independent bound
or complete the original research protocol.

**Work reduction is validated on the larger valid synthetic LLVM exports.
Native elapsed time and Zen 5 performance are unmeasured.** The valid
representative `hash.c` control stays below the declared 1% effect threshold.
The attempted unity, `object.c` and `bitcode.c` exports have pre-existing
independent LLVM reader failures and are excluded from qualification. No
native default-path speedup, typical-workload dominance, general compiler
speedup or broader issue closure is claimed.

## Identity, consumer and growth checks

The twenty-cell original #1362 size/width/control grid was compiled by both
arms in both `-ffrontend-ssa` and `-fno-frontend-ssa` forms: forty comparisons.
Every `.bc` was byte-identical. Independent Clang 23.1.1 consumed the
candidate artifacts, and an independently compiled original-C observer
checked zero initialization plus writes/reads of every global member.
Eight additional 32/128/512/2,048-size distinct/identical exports were also
byte-identical and independently consumed. Thus **48 qualified artifact
comparisons** passed. The valid `hash.c` export separately matches byte-for-byte
and verifies under LLVM for both arms.

The private registered seam also covers zero operands, different codes and
arities, independent identical storage, first/last-word changes, high bits,
reversed duplicate queries, growth and 128 keys forced to one full hash.
For normal two-word and 129-word keys, 128 -> 2,048 rows is 16x input:

| Shape | Width | Probes at 128 | Probes at 2,048 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Late distinct | 2 | 756 | 12,546 |
| Early distinct | 2 | 695 | 11,891 |
| Identical | 2 | 384 | 6,144 |
| Late distinct | 129 | 751 | 12,095 |
| Early distinct | 129 | 782 | 12,104 |
| Identical | 129 | 384 | 6,144 |

Each row makes three queries per input: first admission, reverse replay and
forward replay. Counts include every examined lookup, insertion and rehash
slot. The forced-collision control validates exact identities through growth;
it deliberately makes no linearity assertion.

Resource tradeoff: on this 64-bit build the type row grows from 16 to 24 bytes
and each hash slot is four bytes. Arena growth retains old buffers. The
2,048-distinct artifact has 2,065 wire type records; the existing 16..4,096
row capacities add 65,408 retained hash bytes, and the new 64..8,192 slot
capacities add 65,280 bytes, totaling **130,688 added table bytes**. This is an
allocation-size model, not measured peak RSS. General storage growth remains
linear in distinct keys; no cache-layout or microarchitecture claim is made.
