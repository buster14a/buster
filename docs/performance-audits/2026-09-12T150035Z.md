## Evidence preservation and acceptance blockers

The archived evidence has 2,652,039,127 bytes and SHA-256
f9e32c9acdb136acb65fdb5dddfb67720ec035914b06fe426420d1a069c3867a.
The nine ordered parts and per-part hashes are recorded in

frozen inputs and binaries, joined ledgers, prior failed attempts and self-host
artifacts. Large individual trace/program files were losslessly gzip-compressed;
restore their original names by decompressing their .gz files. The parts were
concatenation-hash verified and saved successfully. Final AArch64 validation is
recorded separately in the implementation PR because it postdates this archive.

No performance acceptance measurements have been made in this checkpoint. Before
such measurements, the proposed non-inferiority limits are: compiler wall time
+2%, peak RSS +2%, generated code bytes +1%, generated runtime +3%, measured on
matched frozen inputs with an upper 95% confidence bound inside each limit. A
per-workload compiler-time or RSS regression above +5% also fails. These are an
engineering proposal, not a maintainer-approved budget; no approval is asserted.
The final workload/target/CPU scope and exclusive 9700X ownership remain unresolved.

Retirement remains blocked by general inline assembly, wider/split vector behavior,
AArch64 f128/conversion/sign handling, baseline-unresolved inputs and earlier fatal
stops, frozen host/resource headers, representative external inputs and extra CPU
profiles, complete differential/platform/sanitizer/native ABI/TLS/unwind/atomic
acceptance, canonical bootstrap, and approved budgets on the exclusive host.
Missing cJSON/LZ4/yyjson/doom/stb or target headers remain setup gaps, not unsupported
program classifications. Hosted green throughput intervals are not budget approval.

Production direct selection, per-function fallback, NONE's direct meaning and
unreachable direct-only deletion are deliberately not claimed complete. Removing
these before the listed supported gaps and acceptance gates pass would break
supported programs and violate the retirement ordering. #36 must remain open.
