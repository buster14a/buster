## Workload

Every arm compiles the same input, the base revision's unity self-host source,
from the base checkout:

```sh
ide cc -Isrc -I<base build>/generated -DBUSTER_UNITY_BUILD=1 -DBUSTER_INCLUDE_TESTS=0 \
  -target x86_64-unknown-linux -march=znver5 -g0 {-fsyntax-only | -c -o <object>} \
```

## The change

- Under `-fc-ast-pilot`, the driver passes the tree to lowering
  (`CIRLowerOptions.syntax_tree`).
- `c_ir_root_conditional` maps its range to the root expression node of the
  body being lowered (`c_ast_spans_locate`):
  - a `CONDITIONAL` splits at its `?` anchor and at the `:` after its middle
    operand;
  - a GNU `CONDITIONAL_OMITTED` splits at its `?` and the `:` after it;
  - a `COMMA` root, and a range that maps to no node, keep the token rule;
  - every other node answers no.
- The span index the lookup needs is the one the tree typer's eager pass
  already builds for every body. The caller passes a `CAstSpanStore`
  (`CParserResult.ast_spans`). The typer then keeps each body's span arrays
  in the analysis arena instead of body scratch, and lowering reads them
  through `CIRLowerOptions.syntax_tree_spans`. The span rules moved from the
  typer's body record into a shared `CAstSpans`.

## Measured runs that failed first

The budgets above stayed fixed through two failed candidates. Both runs are
recorded here.

1. **`4bcda146`** (sha256 `9f979e38716a4133`). Lowering built its own span
   index per body (`c_ast_spans_build`) on the body's first query.
   - C − B was +158.78 M (+0.692%) on `-c`, and +65.62 M (+0.698%) on
     `-fsyntax-only`.
   - The shared span rule had two callers, so it was no longer inlined into
     the typer's eager pass. On `-fsyntax-only` it cost 83.9 M out of line,
     where the pass shrank by only 32.3 M.
   - The builds cost about 120 Ir per spanned node, with 1,034,661 nodes
     spanned. That is more than the 145 M of token scans they replaced.
   - Objects and the default path passed (D − A +0.010% on `-c`).
2. **`934d42ab`** (sha256 `375c3b31055ac765`). The typer kept its spans for
   lowering, and the rule was forced inline (`BUSTER_INLINE`) because
   lowering still kept a build for bodies without kept spans.
   - `-c` passed at −50.24 M (−0.219%).
   - `-fsyntax-only` failed at +10.96 M (+0.117%). The forced inline changed
     the eager pass's code generation by about 8 Ir per node, spread over its
     lines (`c_ast_types_type_body` 216.6 M → 227.2 M), with no added work.

The final candidate drops lowering's own build: every lowered body of the
self-host has a kept index. The rule's only caller is the typer again, as on
base.

## Memory

Peak RSS of the pilot's `-c` (three native runs each, `wait4` `ru_maxrss`)
rises from 695,188–695,324 KB in B to 723,992–724,496 KB in C. That is
**+28.8 MB (+4.1%)**: the kept span arrays, 12 bytes per node of a body's
interval and 4 per body token, held until the unit ends. No RSS budget was
declared for this slice. The cost is reported, not judged. `-fsyntax-only`
keeps nothing, because the driver passes no store without lowering.
