## Refit

In both runs `tiny` is fastest at 2 workers (1.148× and 1.094×) and slower at
4 (1.105× and 1.041×); the capped 4-worker points sit at about 1.0×. A floor
that caps `tiny`'s 75.6 KB of estimated work at 2 workers must exceed 25.2 KB
(three workers) and not exceed 37.8 KB (two). The new floor is 32 KiB:
`COMPILER_DRIVER_LANE_WORK_PER_WORKER_BYTES` = 32 KiB.

Every `equal`, `count`, `size` and `skewed` TU carries at least one 48 KB unit
of work, so no other measured row has less than 32 KiB per worker and none
changes. The cap still never goes below two workers.

The next validation run must show `tiny` at 7 and 13 workers reporting
`lane_gate=capped` with 2 workers, at or above 1.0×, and every other row
unchanged.
