## The change

Under the pilot the eager pass types a body before its queries run, and the
`&` and cast rows those queries intern do not exist yet. A node declined only
for a missing interned row is now marked late, and a query that maps to it,
when the decision declines, re-runs that kind's rule against the live
interning log and decides again. Nothing is interned earlier and nothing is
appended; the re-check's result is not kept.

## Reading: the re-check saves less than layout noise

- **The saved work is small.** The machine runs the re-checks replace are the
  cheapest queries a body makes. The machine runs called from
  `c_parse_expression_type_query` fall by 2,571, and their inclusive Ir by
  only 4.86 M (217.24 M → 212.38 M), about 1,900 instructions each.
  `c_type_parse_machine_run` falls by 5.00 M inclusive in all.
- **The charge is mostly code layout.** `c_ast_types_retype` costs 1.27 M
  inclusive. The rest is `c_parse_expression_type_query`, into which
  `c_ast_types_answer` inlines: +9.21 M inclusive. A per-instruction
  Callgrind dump (`--dump-instr=yes`) of a sibling variant put that charge on
  about 12 extra instructions on each of the 859,291 queries that map to a
  node. They are the same decision compiled differently (flags computed with
  `setne`/`sete` instead of branches), not added work: the source added one
  test on the declined path only.
- **Every variant measured** (C − B, same base and arms):

| Variant | C − B | Answers over B |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| re-type the late node and its ancestors, through a second caller of `c_ast_types_type_node` (`9cf97414`) | +47.55 M | +2,742 |

  Re-typing a late node's ancestors gave 171 more answers. Each way of doing
  it added a second caller of the per-kind rules, and the optimizer then took
  those rules out of line in the eager loop, which runs over every
  expression node. The kept variant re-runs only the late kind's own rule.
- **The verdict.** The pilot budget fails by +0.003%, inside the run-to-run
  spread of the variants above, and the declared budget is not moved after
  the fact. The re-check is exact: it is held to the machine over the corpus
  and the frontend sources, and objects are identical in every arm. But on
  this host it buys no instructions, because the queries it answers cost the
  machine about 1,900 instructions each.
