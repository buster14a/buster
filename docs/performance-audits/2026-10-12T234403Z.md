## Changes

1. **Lazy pool and name indexes** (`568c63fc`).
   - The string pool is copied one generated chunk at a time, on the first
     serial read of a string in it.
   - The iclass and iform indexes, which no compile queries, decode on first
     read.
   - `prewarm_all_forms` completes both before a gang.
2. **Symbol-table prototype** (`6adf768d`).
   - The predefined symbol range is derived once per process into a static
     prototype: on the second serial table, or in `c_prewarm`. Later tables
     copy it.
   - The first table still seeds itself, so a one-compile process does no extra
     work.
   - Capacities, allocation order and ids are unchanged.

## Negative result

Hoisting `select_form`'s coverage and execution-mode filters ahead of
candidate normalization is equivalent, but it saved only 8 K Ir on the
compile+link and cost 0.75 K on the empty unit. Few candidates fail those
filters; most are rejected later by feature or operand checks. Not adopted.

## Handoffs and limits

- **#1125.** The linker stub and loader-finalizer encodes (≈4.8 M Ir per
  dynamic link) and the NOP and forwarding recipes (≈1.9 M) are fixed shapes
  that run the generic selector in every process. They fit #1125's
  candidate D, which is held for 9700X evidence, so they are not changed
  here. #3796's pins move the first XOR/MOV normalization out of codegen,
  so on a compile+link part of that cost would reappear in the linker's
  first XOR/MOV selection. This has not been measured.
- **Predefined object-text macros.** These remain per compile, at about
  2,250 Ir each. Batching their lexing needs token-flag and spelling-space
  equivalence and is not attempted here.
- **Not run:** Zen 5 measurement, sanitized builds, GCC, Zig, MSVC, macOS,
  Windows and `BUSTER_SINGLE_THREADED`. All wall times come from one shared
  4-vCPU VM.
