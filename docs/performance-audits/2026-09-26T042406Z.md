`2026-09-26T042406Z` (Linux x86_64, 4-vCPU Firecracker cloud container (Xeon @
2.80GHz, AVX-512), Clang 18.1.3; **braced case bodies ending in break kept a

## Decision

Direct frontend SSA decided whether a block parameter is trivial from every
incoming edge, including edges out of unreachable blocks. A braced statement
that ends in `break`, `goto`, `return` or `continue` always leaves such a block:
the compound-statement walker pre-creates the continuation after the closing
brace, nothing branches to it, and the next `case` or label still gets a
fall-through edge from it. That edge carries the dead block's unfilled
parameter. So every local read after the label, and everything downstream
including the join after the switch, kept a parameter per case. The canonical
`case OP_X: { ... break; }` interpreter (SQLite's `sqlite3VdbeExec` shape)
paid for a merge of its whole live state at every opcode. FAST and QUALITY
then spilled those cross-block values into homes of their own.

`c_ir_ssa_finish` now decides triviality from the edges out of reachable
blocks. Edges out of unreachable blocks decide only when no reachable edge
carries a value, as before, so a function with no unreachable block makes
exactly the old decisions. MIR dominance already ignores dead edges into the
entry component (`machine.c`, dominance roots), so no downstream contract
changes.

This is a **generated-code and compile-work reduction on a specific, common
shape**, not a whole-compiler throughput result. On Buster's own unity source
it is a small win. No elapsed-time or 9700X result is claimed: the approved
`9700x-service-dispatch.yml` route has never run on GitHub.

## Falsified and rejected alternatives

- **Branching-CFG spill-home coloring (FAST `linear_cfg` gate).**
  - Lifting the gate with the existing dense closure cut the `rec300` frame to 23,200 B on its own, but aborted the 4,000-case interpreter with an arena reservation failure (`arena.h:191`): its planes are `blocks × ⌈homes/64⌉` words.
  - A sparse per-home closure, combined with this change, made frames larger: `rec300` 11,216 → 17,184 B and `interp 1000` 34,888 → 52,440 B. The closure's equal-point rule (every reload before every spill) widens homes that the existing block-local rule colors tightly.
  - Both patches are in [`negative/`](evidence/2026-09-26T042406Z/negative).
  - An independent review found two prerequisites for any version of this. First, `returns_twice` is recognized only by name (`ir_call_returns_twice`); its `t6` counterexample did not miscompile here. Second, debug locations outlive reused homes; that was reproduced on unmodified `main` and filed as #1375.
- **`MADV_HUGEPAGE` on arena reservations.** An `LD_PRELOAD` experiment on the stage-1 compile cut faults 284 k → 5.4 k, but raised system time roughly five-fold, from direct compaction and zeroing of sparse 2 MiB pages, and raised RSS from 1.10 GB to 1.96 GB. Rejected. The 9700X host runs THP `[always]` (`2026-09-19T222922Z`), so its sys-time share does not represent `madvise` hosts.
- **Switch-overlap quadratic and per-invocation x86 prewarm.** Reproduced, but already owned by #954 and #1295 / #1317. Not pursued.

## Limits

One container, noisy for time; no timing was taken. The real-world inputs are
synthetic reductions of interpreter shapes, not the upstream projects: SQLite,
Lua, CPython and QuickJS could not be fetched. The frame-size effect on
Buster's own source is small, because its style rarely ends braced case bodies
with `break`.
