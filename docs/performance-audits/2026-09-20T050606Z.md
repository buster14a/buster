## Build-path control and superseded conclusions

A same-source comparison at main `4b2041c4` across the original two build roots
changed aggregate-ABI timing by -3.306% / -3.068%, despite identical normalized
Clang flags and identical `.text` size (0x00b3ba6a). `.text` began at 0x0f61430
versus 0x0f613e0: an 80-byte displacement. Several inspected functions retained
their sizes and shared that displacement. This is evidence of a build/layout
confound, not proof of its complete microarchitectural cause. A/A with one
immutable binary does not detect it. Do not subtract these control percentages
from other comparisons.

This supersedes the performance-acceptance conclusions of
[2026-09-20T023154Z](2026-09-20T023154Z.md) and
[2026-09-20T030551Z](https://github.com/buster14a/buster/blob/4a79ed10/docs/performance-audits/2026-09-20T030551Z.md).
Those immutable audits retain their historical observations, but comparisons
across their build roots cannot establish isolated source-change benefit.
Earlier sparse-promotion, lazy-map/publication and compact-cache experiments
were exploratory and did not justify acceptance. Only the fresh fixed plan
below determines the decisions reported here.

Each consecutive AB/BA pair of pairs forms one geometric-ratio block (20 blocks).

sorted ratios. The per-row failure bound is 2*(1+20+190)/2^20; a union bound over

independent blocks. Eight crossbuild-control rows are separate diagnostic data.

[analyze.py](evidence/2026-09-20T050606Z/analyze.py) reproduces all tabulated rows.

synthetic corpus, warm filesystem/new process, no physical-isolation attestation,
and possible serial dependence. Powersave/balance_performance settings were
preserved. Matching roots removes the observed path mismatch, but source-induced
layout changes remain part of these binary results. No new DRAM-fill/cycle survey,
Zen 4 replication, or formal native-retirement service admission was performed.
