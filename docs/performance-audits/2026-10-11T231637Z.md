Every `.c` file in Lua's `src/` and zlib's top level is compiled alone, and
SQLite as its one amalgamated unit: 50 units per cell.

This container has no Windows SDK, so the CRT and Win32 headers are Ubuntu's
`mingw-w64-x86-64-dev` 11.0.1-3build1 (mingw-w64 runtime license, mostly
public domain or ZPL-2.1 per the package's `debian/copyright`, not
per-file verified). They are used only to preprocess and are not part of any
output. Getting them through Buster needed a scratch overlay ahead of them,
and nothing in this repository changed:

- `psdk_inc/intrin-impl.h` replaced by its prototypes only, because its
  `__INTRINSIC_PROLOG` conditionals are rejected
  ([#3681](https://github.com/buster14a/buster/issues/3681)) and its GCC
  `__builtin_ia32_*` bodies cannot be lowered;
- `intrin.h` and the `*mmintrin.h`/`x86intrin.h` family stubbed to the
  `__m128` vector typedefs;
- `Windows.h` forwarding to `windows.h` (case-sensitive host).

The compile is `ide cc -v {-g0 | -g} -c -target {x86_64 | aarch64}-windows-msvc
-nostdlibinc -isystem OVERLAY -isystem /usr/x86_64-w64-mingw32/include
-D__declspec=__declspec` (`-DSQLITE_OMIT_SEH` for SQLite, which otherwise uses
SEH `__try`). `-D__declspec=__declspec` keeps mingw's `__GNUC__` path from
undefining `__MINGW_IMPORT`. The AArch64 cells reuse the x86-64 mingw headers;
they parse, and these units do not depend on the difference.

S is the COFF header section count and R the sum of the section headers'
relocation counts (with the overflow marker read). `relocation_visits` is the
`OBJECT_WRITE` ledger from `ide cc -v`.

## Reading

On representative Windows-target output the per-section sweeps cost
0.2%–1.1% of compile instructions; #3516 removes about two thirds of the
writer's instructions there. The residual was large only when priority groups
multiplied the section count (59.6% of compile Ir at G = 512 in
`2026-10-11T150202Z`). Both are instruction counts on a shared VM: no wall
time was measured here, and no speedup is claimed without the 9700X.

The existing 9700X routes do not reach this path: `compiler-compare-v1` and
`throughput-corpus-v2` compile Buster's own unity source and the corpus for
the host (ELF), and a direct workload compiles alone against system headers,
so it cannot invoke the compiler. A 9700X measurement of the COFF writer needs
a COFF cell (for example the header-free priority-group generator of
`2026-10-11T150202Z`) in a trusted profile on `main`.
