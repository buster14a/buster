## The instrument

The instrument runs at the first stage of every aggregate segment frame while
the pilot's specifier index exists, and prints one `PLAINCENSUS` row per frame.
It contains #3842's two predicates:
- **Token predicate.** Every token in `[frame->start, frame->end)` is an
  identifier, a preprocessing number, `*`, `[`, `]` or `,`. No identifier spells
  a decoration word: the GNU, `__declspec` and asm attribute spellings,
  `__extension__`, `_Static_assert`/`static_assert`, the `_Alignas`/`alignas`
  and `aligned` spellings, and `vector_size`.
- **Tree test.**
  1. The word-only `DECL_SPECIFIERS` list must sit at the member's first token
     (`c_parse_specifier_node`). None there gives `why=1`.
  2. Its words may not be `__extension__`, alignas, attribute, declspec, asm
     or static_assert. A decoration word gives `why=3`.
  3. The nodes up to `MEMBER_DECLARATION` may only be:
     - a `MEMBER_DECLARATOR` with presence exactly 1;
     - a `DECLARATOR_POINTER` without bit 0;
     - `DECLARATOR_ARRAY` or `DECLARATOR_NAME`;
     - `NUMBER`, `CHARACTER` or `IDENTIFIER`;
     - an arithmetic or bitwise operator.

     Any other kind gives `why=2`.
  4. No anchor token may spell a decoration word (`why=3`).

  A disagreeing row also prints the member's tokens, and, when the tree admits
  the member, its `c_ast_dump`.
