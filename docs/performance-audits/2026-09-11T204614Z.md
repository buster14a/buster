## Change and correctness

Source `369877c8bb42094ea7a765b596a4a1fbaaf43ac2`, tree
`472eb2e39ba433d86c5429d9d587834d3f813466`, removes unnecessary queries from
the opt-in pipeline in #40. FOLD/ADDRESS reject irrelevant opcodes before
querying operand constants and types; constant lookup checks its defining
opcode before its width; DCE checks for an unused result before its purity.

certification boundary are unchanged. An independent review checked this
preservation. This does not bypass compaction or weaken validation.

## Result and remaining acceptance

The query reduction's paired wall-time estimates include -1.74% for control
flow/FAST and -1.89% for backend pressure/FAST, with mixed small signs in other
cases. These remain inconclusive; no speedup is claimed.

The optimized all-enabled pipeline versus off still shows **+6.53%** for
control flow/FAST and **+6.77%** for backend pressure/FAST. Disabling folding
relative to all-enabled changes those two estimates by -4.09% and -4.45%.
The other disabled-pass estimates are mixed and small: address +0.30%/-0.85%,
DCE +0.13%/+1.60%, and parameters +2.51%/-0.76%, respectively. These
ablations identify a cost to investigate; they do not justify picking a
default from noisy point estimates. The complete tables preserve every case.

**Keep the pipeline opt-in. #40's total-time acceptance remains unresolved.**
The implementation and independent controls are reviewable, but neither
lower row counts nor passing the large-regression guard completes that
criterion. The default remains OFF. A qualified A/A-controlled acceptance
series, including the other allocator-mode ablations, remains outstanding.
