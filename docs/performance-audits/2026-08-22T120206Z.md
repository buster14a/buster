into their final array instead of staging ranges that never coalesced**).
Takes the preprocessor lead `2026-08-22T084855Z` ranked third and
`2026-08-22T091548Z` left untaken: the token materialization at
`c_source.c:6391`, 5,50% of stage-1 DRAM fills, "the lexer writing into the
final array at best".  This entry is that fix in its achievable form — the
*preprocessor* writes every output token into its final slot exactly once —
plus the population measurement that had to come first, which found the
staging machinery worse than the survey knew.

**The population, probe-counted over the unity stage-1 compile** (temporary
counters in `c_preprocess`, removed after the run): 269.056 text lines produce
2.936.901 output tokens (the official `tokens to the parser` is 2.936.752; the
extra 149 are the probe's own source, which stage 1 compiles).  1.877.911 of
those tokens — 64% — come from the 226.299 lines that name no macro and pass
straight through; 42.757 expansion lines take 684.316 input tokens to
1.058.990 output tokens (×1,55).  The decisive number: **268.977 range appends
created 268.977 range nodes — the coalescing in
`c_preprocess_output_append_range` never fired once**, because the range node
itself was allocated between one line's staging array and the next, so no two
ranges were ever adjacent.  The "few bulk copies" the structure's comment
promised were one line-sized `memcpy` per line (median line ~11 tokens,
~131 bytes), driven by a 269 K-node pointer chase over 6,5 MB of nodes, after
every straight-through token had already been copied once from the lexed
array into its staging line.

**What blocked writing tokens to their final place, and what unblocked it.**
The final count is unknown until the last line lands (macro expansion decides
it), and the output array cannot grow inside the caller's arena because every
other preprocessing allocation — macro tables, expansion nodes, map regions,
staging — interleaves between appends.  Both blocks fall to the treatment the
spelling space has used since it existed: a private commit-on-demand arena
(1 GB reserved, pages commit on touch), whose tail is the stream.  Nothing
else allocates from it, so successive reservations are contiguous and the
base is the finished `result.tokens` the moment the last line lands.  There
is no reordering to defeat: both output paths only ever append.  The stronger
form recorded in the survey — the *lexer* writing the final array — stays
impossible for a real reason worth recording: the lexed stream still carries
the newlines, directive lines, and inactive-conditional regions the
preprocessor consumes, and expansion rewrites what remains, so finality
simply does not exist at lex time.  The preprocessor's line loop is the first
point that knows which tokens survive, and that is where the write now
happens.

**The shape.**  `CTokenStream` replaces `CPreprocessTokenRange`: a line
reserves its upper bound at the arena tail (its lexed token count, or the
expansion's node count), writes the tokens that survive the newline or
pragma strip, and hands the surplus slots back
(`arena_set_position`, the `c_space_shrink` idiom).  A no-expansion line —
the 64% — now streams straight from the lexed array; its staging copy is
gone, and the macro scan and the expansion wrap read `lex.tokens` directly.
An expanded line materializes its node list into the stream tail instead of
a scratch array.  The range list, its append/coalesce helper, and the final
269 K-piece copy are deleted outright.  The arena rides in
`CSourceMapRecovery` beside the spelling arena with the same lifetime
contract; the bench loop destroys both.

The DRAM return is *larger* than the line's 5,50% share — the opposite
surprise from `2026-08-22T091548Z`'s selector lead, and the same lesson read

the node walk were spread across the profile, and removing the structure
removed them all.  A miss share says where a line's traffic goes; what a fix
returns is decided by how many times the same bytes cross the hierarchy, and
this structure crossed three times where once sufficed.
