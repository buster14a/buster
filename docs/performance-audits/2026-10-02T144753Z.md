## Selected mechanism and bounds

`c_analyze_semantics_core` previously allocated 16,384 aggregate-tag slots for
every translation unit, including scalar-only and anonymous-aggregate-only
source. A `CAggregateLookupSlot` has a current 64-bit size of 32 bytes: the
portable logical allocation is `16384 * sizeof(CAggregateLookupSlot)`, or
524,288 bytes on that layout. `arena_allocate_zeroed` clears only the overlap
with dirty arena storage; fresh OS-zeroed pages do not pay that explicit fill.
The change removes the allocation from tag-free units and therefore avoids its
dirty clear when it would have occurred. Logical bytes are not RSS, physical
zeroing, cache misses, retired instructions or a timing result.

Keep the stable `CAggregateLookup` header complete and empty until its first
unqualified tag owner. The first admitted insertion uses the unchanged 16,384
capacity and zeroed allocation; subsequent capacities double at half occupancy.
Qualified aliases and anonymous aggregates allocate no slots. Complete empty
headers certify absence. Failed initial admission makes the empty header
incomplete and invokes the existing scope-aware fallback without probing a null
array. Speculative rollback retains the allocated header/array; live type-row
validation rejects stale and repurposed IDs as before. The protected-query
one-slot incomplete index is unchanged.

Both production readers now handle zero capacity. Full source inspection of
`c_gen.c` confirmed the lowering consumer honors `decided`; null/zero-sized
protected-query snapshot ranges already skip retention validation. No pointer
ownership crosses a released arena and no new dependency, concurrency, IR,
generated binding or native-retirement authority/policy is introduced.

## Rejected alternatives and follow-ups

A broad token/IR layout replacement was not justified by current populations and
would overlap existing work. The assembly printer's per-code-byte label flags
could be packed, but that lower-priority option has no measured workload share
and was not implemented. Tagged translation units still allocate the original
table; this change does not claim a reduction for them, nor a global 512 KiB RSS
improvement.

Two independent source findings were published as unclaimed follow-ups:

- [#2349](https://github.com/buster14a/buster/issues/2349): scope-index construction
  cursors remain retained after their last fill/sort use; source-derived logical
  bound `4 * scope_count` bytes plus alignment.
- [#2350](https://github.com/buster14a/buster/issues/2350): repeated variadic-call
  plans allocate 56-byte shapes and 16-byte placements per argument; synchronous
  consumers allow bounded workspace reuse, with allocation-growth tradeoffs.

this audit. Qualified hardware performance acceptance remains pending and is
