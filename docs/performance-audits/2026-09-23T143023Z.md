## Decision

The current immutable-binary A/A reader cannot measure the build-root confound
observed in the [#791 matched-build audit](2026-09-20T050606Z.md): identical

candidate verdict.

## Method and provenance

The repository base was `194bd65b2d6c2668d3ccecd940af846927b8e487`.
`tools/zen5_build_control.py` reuses the existing fixed 120-pair, two-round
label/path/order schedule. It adds separate immutable A/B build records, serial
build timing, same-source/toolchain/environment checks, root-normalized argv
and complete command-manifest digests, binary and `.text` identities, and a
per-child binary hash at each observed slot. Same-root rebuild and cross-root
captures have distinct declared kinds. All invalid observations remain in the
record; the analysis is descriptive only. The ordinary throughput CI guard and
the approved native-retirement statistical policy are unchanged.

The offline reader verifies internal consistency, not that an authenticated
service actually produced the bytes, that retained command manifests match
their claimed digests, or that a declared family was published before samples.
The protected service and independent bundle replay must establish those
relations before #426 can use a physical capture.
