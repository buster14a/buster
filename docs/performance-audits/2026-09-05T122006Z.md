`2026-09-05T122006Z` (Linux x86_64, Tiger Lake i7-1185G7 as an AVX-512 stand-
in for Zen 5; **AVX-512/Zen 5 throughput audit of the whole pipeline: five
frontend patches land at -1,84%, the 562 M per-invocation metadata prewarm
floor, and a measured plan for selection, allocation, encoding, IR and the
frontend**).

The brief was to prepare every hot codepath — instruction selection, both
register allocators, scheduling, encoding, the canonical IR and the C
frontend — for high-throughput AVX-512 on Zen 5's native 512-bit datapath,
with data structures and algorithms open to change. The session opened
census-first, in the discipline this file prescribes: a fresh stage-1 profile
on the reference host, then fifteen independent read-only audits, one per
subsystem, each pricing its findings against that profile and against the
do-not-retry lists of the eighty audits before it, then a ranking and an
adversarial verification pass, then patches in isolated worktrees measured
one at a time. This entry records what was measured, what landed, what the
readers found, and what remains.

**Host and method.** The Zen 5 development box was not available; the
measurements are on an i7-1185G7 (Tiger Lake, AVX-512 F/BW/VL/VBMI/VBMI2, 4
cores) with the clang-built Release `ide` (`-march=native`). Instructions
retired (`instructions:u`) is the currency throughout, as this file requires;
wall time on a loaded 4-core laptop is not quoted. Every A/B compiles ONE
frozen tree (`git archive HEAD` of `b3852d98`) with two saved compiler
binaries and the same `-o`, three alternating pairs, preceded by a `cmp` of
the two outputs. Two traps paid for here, recorded so the next session does
not pay again: the stage-1 compile compiles the *current* tree, so once the
compiler's own sources are edited the "after" output legitimately differs
from the "before" output (a 20-line `c_gen.c` patch moved the executable by
168 bytes) — byte identity is only meaningful over a frozen tree; and the
stage-1 command needs `-lm` since `b6d4da0c`, without which the link fails on
`floorf` after code generation and the count silently omits the link.

**The per-invocation floor, which is the headline for every build that is
not a unity build.** `ide cc -c` of `int main(void){return 0;}` costs
**562 M instructions**, and 89,7% of it is `buster_x86_metadata_prewarm`
(~504 M): `buster_x86_metadata_decode_tables_once` 55,5% (~312 M — the
1,7 MB string pool copied one byte at a time through a chunk division and
two bounds checks per byte, and every form/operand/coverage/range record
assembled field by field through the per-byte base64 group decoder, ~40
instructions per byte), then normalizing and pattern-parsing all 11.013
forms (`buster_x86_metadata_emit_parse_pattern` 17,1%,
`buster_x86_metadata_normalized_form` 12,3%, `validate_form_record` 9,5%)
although the encoder ever touches a few hundred of them, then
`machine_x86_64_exact_prewarm` 9,4% (the 64+9 dense tables authored through
the metadata encoder, two emissions per 16x16 member) and the shape-cache
prewarm 3,4%. The AGENTS.md sentence that "no part of the compile path
queries [x86 metadata]" is false as of this tree: `codegen_prewarm_for_target`
runs the full decode on every x86-64 module, and ~3,0% of stage 1 (~340 M)
still runs *per row* through `buster_x86_metadata_emit_exact_machine`
(0,40% directly from the encoder's exact-sequence steps, 0,33% via the
string-mnemonic bridge `machine_x64_emit_metadata_instruction` for every
prologue, epilogue, `CALL`, `JCC`/`JMP` and `SWITCH` row, 0,18% via the
`machine_x64_emit_exact_recipe` fallback) — 1.300-2.700 instructions per
encode for byte shapes that are completely fixed. A 100-file build pays
56 G instructions of prewarm before it compiles a line. The decode-side fix landed, in
three measured steps on the frozen tree: the pool copied chunk-wise with
`memcpy` (-32,4 M); every base64 blob decoded whole — a 256-entry table
scalar kernel with no division, and under AVX-512 VBMI a 64-characters-per-
iteration kernel (`vpmovb2m` rejects non-ASCII, `vpermi2b` maps the
characters through the two table halves, two `vpmultishiftqb` reads pull the
8-bit windows, `vpternlogq` selects, `vpermb` packs 48 bytes, a masked store
— 10 instructions per 48 output bytes, 64/64 lanes at the lookup, 48/64 at
the pack, the 4:3 ratio of the encoding) — with flat record readers that
mirror the generated `*_at` readers and a startup self-check against them
(-224,3 M more); and the prewarm no longer normalizes and pattern-parses all
11.013 forms but prepares each on its first serial touch — a stage-1 compile
touches 1.231 of them, a tiny compile 1.052 (-156,8 M more). Together
**-413,4 M (-3,68%) on stage 1, and the one-line compile falls from 581 M
to 165 M instructions (-71,6%)**, byte-identical, with a permanent test that
checks every pool byte and every blob byte through both kernels against the
generated per-byte accessors. One contract came out of it and is documented
in the header: `ide cc` never calls `lane_run` — the compile is serial end to
end — so lazy per-form preparation is sound under the serial-initialization
check; a worker gang that queries metadata must call
`buster_x86_metadata_prewarm_all_forms()` first, as the test harness now
does. What remains of the floor (165 M) is validation's twenty string hashes
per form, the NUL-distance loop and the shape-cache prewarm. The
encoder-side fix landed and is the
largest single result of the session: **prewarm-proven byte templates for
the fixed-shape rows, -529,0 M (-4,71%) on stage 1** (11.229,80 ->
10.700,84 M standalone, three pairs, byte-identical, 313.510 unit
assertions). The census that ranked it counted 417.400 rows or sequence
steps per stage 1 reaching `machine_x64_emit_exact_form`: JMP 13,5%,
SETCC's setne+movzx steps 13,4%, JCC's jne+jmp and je+jmp pairs 23%,
LEA_SYMBOL 9,7%, the epilogue POPs 7,8%, CALL rel32 7,7%, UDIV's xor+div
3,7%, the prologue and SWITCH rows the rest. The fix follows the
2026-08-18f pattern one level up: a 16-byte `MachineX64FixedTemplate`
(bytes, count, patch offset and width) per fixed shape — 153 static rows
for the prologue/epilogue/call/switch/divide instructions and 1.536 dynamic
rows for every recipe-tail variant and sequence step whose operands are all
fixed kinds (a 16-row family per GPR operand) — published once at
`machine_x86_64_exact_prewarm` through the very bridge it replaces, with
each patched row proven by a second emission under a different field value
and retired on any byte difference outside the field. Per row the encoder
copies the template and patches the field; the bridge stays as the
fail-closed fallback for an unpublished row, and the exact-attempt counters
keep their invariants. What stays on the bridge is cold by the census
(64-bit SWITCH immediates, the XMM bridges, the FCMP parity swap, TLS, the
variadic and atomic expansions, STACK_ALLOCATE — under 0,4% of calls
together). The reason the win exceeds the 340 M attributed to the metadata
encoder is that the mnemonic ladder, the shape hashing, the physical-operand
construction and the sequence steps' operand projection all went with it.

**What landed in-tree first (all five byte-identical on the frozen tree, all
313.508 unit assertions and 39 modules passing, `test_self_host`
deterministic at 36.017.312 bytes with stage 1 at 11.024,3 M):**

- **Stage 1 — the post-lowering atomic-access walk answers from the opcode
  summary** (`c_ir_atomic_aggregate_accesses_lowerable`, `c_gen.c`). Every
  function's finished body was walked through its 64-byte linked rows to find
  the six atomic accesses in the whole unit; `IrFunction.opcode_summary`
  already tracks `ATOMIC_LOAD`/`ATOMIC_STORE`, so the walk is gated on
  `ir_function_may_contain_opcodes`. Measured with stage 2 together.
- **Stage 2 — the GNU-construct rescan is skipped under GNU dialects**
  (`c_ir_unsupported_gnu_construct`). Every token of every definition was
  rescanned for initializer ranges, `asm goto` and case ranges, but every
  diagnostic the scan can produce is refused only outside the GNU dialects
  (the default is gnu17), so under them the walk can only answer "nothing"
  and now answers it without walking; the token index it left behind is read
  only beside a non-empty message. Stages 1+2: 11.230,77 -> 11.120,33 M,
  **-110,4 M (-0,983%)**, 3/3 pairs.
- **Stage 3 — the callee name index is keyed by interned symbol id**
  (`c_ir_function_name_resolution_symbol`). Every call site hashed the
  callee's spelling (`buster_hash_64` 0,31% self), took a modulo and a string
  compare; the callee token already carries its symbol, so the index gains a
  `symbol_groups[symbol]` column filled at build time (an identity intern per
  function declaration) and the spelling path stays for symbol-0 tokens and
  symbols interned after the build. Cumulative 11.095,67 M, **-135,1 M
  (-1,203%)**; the stage alone -24,7 M.
- **Stage 4 — attribute skipping compares symbol ids** (`c_parse_skip_
  attributes`, with `C_SYMBOL_WELL_KNOWN_EXTENSION`/`DECLSPEC` added to the
  well-known set beside the two attribute spellings). Cumulative 11.086,79 M,
  **-144,0 M (-1,282%)**; the stage alone -8,9 M — smaller than the reader's
  estimate because the ladder ran on identifiers only.
- **Stage 5 — each declaration's specifier set is computed once**
  (`c_declaration_specifier_sets_build`). `c_declaration_well_known_set`
  rescanned a declaration's pre-body tokens from ten sites across the
  lowering's passes; one walk with every well-known bit interesting and a
  mask at each reader is the same answer (bit 0, the empty spelling, stays
  out of the walk). Cumulative 11.023,9 M, **-206,5 M (-1,839%)**, 3/3
  pairs; the stage alone -62,5 M.

**What the fifteen readers established, ranked by what it is worth
(estimates are the readers', priced against this profile; the ones marked
"in flight" have patches being measured in worktrees as this is written):**

- *Selection* (`machine_select_canonical_function_x86_64`, the largest
  symbol) is per-row overhead and data layout, not selection logic: fourteen
  separate per-value arrays (~74 bytes per value, ~85 MB of stores per
  compile, initialised by scalar loops and seven memsets); ~3 M
  `ir_type_from_id` lookups into 136-byte `IrType` rows where the
  `layout.resolved` byte test alone is ~1,8% of the function; ~500 K random
  64-byte definition-row fetches to read one opcode byte; a 16-byte
  `MachineLineMark` generic-stream append plus a source lookup for 1,2 M
  rows; eight side streams through the generic `machine_stream_append`
  (~25-30 instructions each, 0,8% self); a ~1,4 KB `MachineX64CallPlan`
  zeroed per call with 40-byte value shapes recomputed per argument. The
  readers' plan landed at **-202,7 M (-1,81%)**, byte-identical under both
  allocators, measured item by item: typed inline cursors for every side
  stream with `{size, alignment}` slot rows and `{symbol, reference}`
  call-target rows that cannot drift apart, single-chunk immediates and
  switch cases aliased instead of flattened (**-50,4 M**); 8-byte
  `{row, instruction}` line marks written into an exact-count array with
  the source derived at consumption from `instruction_canonical_sources`
  (**-62,7 M**); a 4-byte per-type `MachineTypeClass` projection (flags,
  kind, size log2, bit-width log2) built once per module before selection,
  so `IrType` is fetched only for field offsets and aggregate sizes
  (**-45,2 M**); one arena block for the per-value facts filled by two
  memsets, the operand-walk facts interleaved into a 16-byte record,
  branch fusions keyed per block, the atomic-success map allocated only when
  the opcode summary admits it, and a place-kind byte read by
  LOAD/STORE/FIELD/INDEX/CALL instead of the defining row (**-23,4 M**);
  and a per-signature call plan filled on first call so a call site no
  longer zeroes ~1,4 KB and recomputes its argument shapes (**-20,9 M**).
  Hoisting the vector gate and the `rows ?` indirection measured neutral
  and was kept for the memory traffic alone. Binding traps from the earlier audits: never split or
  peel the opcode switch (+21,5/+32,1 M), never force-inline `select_row`
  (+21,5 M), never add a per-row class test, keep the 24-byte row.
- *FAST allocation* (8,2% inclusive) hides a quadratic: four linear scans
  over `function->edges` inside the block loop (designated-predecessor,
  contract-intersection, retroactive-conform and terminator successor
  lookups) make the scan O(blocks x edges) per function although the selector
  emits edges grouped by destination and the prepass already builds a CSR.
  The reader priced an O(1) per-block edge index at ~250 M and 0,5
  confidence; **it was built, proven byte-identical for both allocators,
  and measured +40,9 M** with the counting folded into the prepass walk
  (clang reshaped the hot operand walk: 560 -> 370 stack references, the
  in-loop code-shape trap of 2026-08-30T182357Z stage 4 again) **and +3,5 M
  as a self-contained build after the walk — a wash, not landed, do not
  retry**: the four scans carry 0,00-0,12% of the placement's samples
  because this corpus's functions have out-degrees of one or two, so
  O(blocks x edges) never bites and the cheapest correct index costs what
  the scans cost. What did land from the same reader: iterating only the
  held lanes of the register-file walks through an occupancy mask derived on
  demand from the existing `vpcmpeqd` free-candidate kernel (the block-entry
  reset, the flush at every call, the eviction walk; `machine_fast_pick`
  0,70% -> 0,13%, **-28,9 M**), and a `wants_quality_facts` gate that keeps
  the interval/disqualified/loop-span facts out of the FAST prepass
  (**-21,1 M**): **-50,0 M (-0,45%)** on FAST, -58,5 M on QUALITY. The lead
  left standing there is the arena commit and memset of the per-function
  `contract_*`/`out_*` arrays (block_count x register_count each, ~9% of
  the placement's samples).
- *Encoding* (8,0% inclusive) is the metadata bridge above plus a ~10-
  instruction dense path for 1,66 M exact rows; the readers priced the
  requested SIMD batch encoder (SoA command stream, template lookups by
  `vpermb`, prefix-summed byte offsets, `vpcompressb` packing) at ~10 M and
  0,3 confidence — the population is already at ~10 instructions per row and
  the 2026-08-18f verdict stands: the win is in the rows that still go
  through the metadata encoder (~340 M), not in vectorizing the dense ones.
- *The initializer folder* is 8,2% inclusive for ~534 K scalar leaves at
  1.000-1.300 instructions each (a temporal arena, a task array, string and
  compound-literal probes, a 140-byte `CIrQueryFrame` copied three times, the
  shunting-yard evaluator, a per-byte store with an endianness test per
  byte), with every leaf re-walking its aggregate's 56-byte `IrField` rows
  four times. Both landed: a per-aggregate slot table (a dense 24-byte row per
  initializable member with unnamed bit-fields excluded and the union rule
  folded in, the three walkers kept as reference and compared row for row
  in debug builds, **-39,9 M**), and a leaf-class byte computed from the
  token kinds and the child's kind before the dispatch, whose out-of-line
  arms reuse the evaluator's own literal routines and reproduce its unary
  and cast semantics, then store once little-endian — 519.545 of the
  546.837 scalar leaves (95%) take it, every other leaf takes the untouched
  folder, and a debug gate re-folds each fast leaf through the legacy path
  and compares bytes (**-332,1 M**, plus **-4,9 M** for enum-constant
  identifiers): **-376,9 M (-3,36%)**, byte-identical. The residue is the
  17,7 K parenthesized macro-expanded leaves (~26 M) and the parser's own
  array-bound inference.
- *String literals* are 45% of the unit's bytes and are decoded byte by byte
  with a branch per byte (`c_ir_decode_quoted` 1,5-2,1%), twice in the
  legacy initializer path (probe, then decode), a third time in the parser's
  array-bound inference which needs only the count, and concatenated through
  a second buffer even for one fragment. The 64-byte-window kernel in the
  `simd.h` vocabulary landed: a masked load under the remaining-length
  prefix, `simd512_equal_byte` against a splatted backslash, a masked
  store of the escape-free window or of the prefix up to the first escape,
  and the verbatim scalar switch for the escape itself (kept as
  `c_ir_decode_quoted_reference` and gated by a differential test over every
  escape kind slid across offsets 0..200, bodies of 0/63/64/65/127/128..4097
  bytes, a 70 KB literal and 3.000 fuzz bodies). On this corpus 414.251
  windows run, 46% escape-free with 89,5% lane utilization, and ~95% of the
  literal bytes move through masked stores: **-239,0 M (-2,13%)** for the
  decoder alone. The count-only variant for the parser's array-bound
  inference, the single-fragment path and the single decode in the legacy
  probe measured within inlining variance of each other (+7,1/-1,0/-0,1 M;
  the count variant's cheapest form is -1,3 M), because once the kernel is
  windowed the ~200-instruction per-call prefix (trim, encoding scan, three
  arena allocations) dominates the 34 K small literals: **-233,1 M (-2,08%)**
  together, byte-identical.
- *The lowering machines* (27% inclusive) re-walk each token range 8-11
  times for a closed set of questions (first top-level `?`, `||`, `,`,
  assignment, statement `;`, is-call, is-cast); the readers' group-facts pass
  (one delimiter-stack walk writing per-token class and next-top-level
  links, ~180 M) is the structural lever; the two cheap-rejection levers
  landed: `c_ir_prepare_calls_discover` tests `tokens[index + 1]` for `(`
  before any per-token classification (914.056 tokens through the loop,
  98.599 candidates — 89% rejected by one byte load, **-93,8 M**), and a
  proven-negative gate plus a per-opener memo stand in front of every
  type-name probe of a parenthesized group (186.584 probes: 20% proven
  negative from the symbol's type-start word bits and the bound entity's
  kind, 59% answered by the memo, 21% still issued; `typeof`-headed groups
  and lone unbound identifiers are never memoized, **-49,2 M**) —
  **-144,0 M (-1,28%)** together, byte-identical.
- *The parser* (~1,2-1,4 G) is the same story in the earlier phase: spelling
  ladders where the token carries its symbol id, `c_parse_skip_attributes`
  called before `statement_start` is tested, a predefined-name ladder run on
  every bound identifier before the undeclared test, names re-interned at
  entity creation, a full scope-chain lookup for `int`, and a
  `CParserStatement` tree that only the static-assert finder reads. All of
  it landed, byte-identical: `c_parse_skip_attributes` runs only at
  statement starts, with a one-byte `[` test in front of the C23 probe
  (**-86,7 M** — it was a per-token call with three spelling compares); the

- *The lexer* is at the floors the 2026-08-22/08-30 audits measured; the only

- *Object writer, linker and DWARF* (~2,0-2,5% together on this `-lm`
  dynamic-ELF path; `object_write_elf64`, the static link and SHA-256 are not

  (~22 M), a per-function CSR replacing the O(locals x scopes) seed scan in
  `debug_variable_add_location` (one 1.608-local function dominates, ~20 M),
  DWARF relocations as per-section offset streams applied as base-add sweeps
  instead of 56-byte records rebased and skip-scanned three times (~15 M),
  and an `IrSymbolId`-indexed object-symbol memo replacing the byte-serial
  name index built twice (~7 M).
- *The 6 MB generated header is the largest single input cost, and the
  cheapest fix is to stop compiling it.* 45% of the unit's bytes are the
  x86 metadata's string pool and base64 blobs spelled as C string literals so
  that `ide cc` can consume them; their lowering (literal decode, initializer
  folding) was most of `c_ir_global_initializer`'s 8,2% before this session's
  patches, and their runtime decode was the 562 M startup floor. The
  driver/OS reader's top item is to ship the tables as decoded binary
  through a C23 `#embed` (or an equivalent build-time binary import the
  frontend lowers as one data image): it deletes both the decode and the
  literal lowering, ~1.100 M by the reader's estimate at 0,45 confidence,
  and is the one proposal here that removes work from the workload rather
  than doing it faster. It needs `#embed` support in the frontend and a
  build step that bakes the tables; it is recorded, not taken.
- *Cross-cutting:* the IR's 64-byte `IrInstruction` with three pointer fields
  and a `next` link, the linked block parameter/predecessor lists, and the
  136-byte `IrType` are the shape every backend consumer pays for; a hot
  16-32-byte row with u32 index-linked operand pools and a u8 kind/size-log2
  type projection is the layout change that makes the selector, the
  validator and codegen's sizing loops stream instead of chase. And the whole
  compile is serial: `lane_run` has no caller in the compiler, so the SPMD
  half of the AGENTS.md parallelism target is unbuilt. The driver/OS reader
  confirmed the codegen loop is already fragment-shaped — selection,
  placement and encoding run in a per-function scratch arena and return
  function-relative bytes, row offsets and call sites; the only shared
  writes are the copy into the module buffer, the relocation/line/debug
  appends with absolute offsets, the statistics, and the canonical fallback
  (which never runs on stage 1) — so per-function fragments merged by
  prefix sum in function order are byte-identical by construction at any
  lane count, for under 10 M instructions of merge. Lowering is
  parallelizable per definition only with per-lane overflow tables for the
  54 `ir_program_add_*` mutation sites and a deterministic renumbering pass;
  preprocessing, the parse, interning, global layout, object writing and
  linking stay serial. Amdahl on 8-16 Zen 5 threads: ~1,3x wall for codegen
  alone, ~2,1-2,8x with function bodies and initializers; the prerequisite
  is that the resident gang keeps workers alive, so every lazily built table
  (the metadata's lazy per-form preparation included) must be prewarmed
  before the first `lane_run`. This is a wall-clock lever, not an
  instructions:u one, and it is recorded, not taken.

**The five patterns behind almost every number above**, as the ranking pass
put them, because they are what the next audit should look for first:
(1) spelling ladders where the token already carries an interned symbol id,
in the parser, the preprocessor, the lowering and the driver's keyword hash;
(2) facts re-derived per query instead of recorded by the pass that already
held them — specifier sets, group facts, line marks, encode capacity,
`has_vector`, definition points, decoded literals; (3) by-value copies of
wide records that no `memcpy` census can see because arena struct stores
never reach libc (`CPpToken` 36 bytes copied 8-10 times, `CIrQueryFrame`
140, `MachineX64CallPlan` 1,4 KB); (4) per-value and per-token side arrays
allocated separately and filled by scalar loops, where one block with a
0xff region and a 0x00 region and two memsets does the same work, with the
rare populations gated by an existing summary; (5) generic containers on hot
paths — the chunked builder stream, the linked block-parameter and macro
chains, the statement tree — against typed cursors and index-linked arrays,
since the fixed point needs the same order, not the same container. One-byte
projections (token shape and class, row opcode, type class, place kind) are
the enabling layout for all of it, and an emitter's cost has to be repaid by
at least two consumers landing with it.

**Left standing, ranked** (the verification pass ran out of budget before
it could adversarially check these, so they carry the readers' confidence,
not a verdict; every estimate is against the *pristine* profile and must be
re-priced on the new one): the `#embed` route for the metadata tables
(~350 M, days) and the range-based macro-expansion engine (~280 M, weeks)

literals across the parser and the lowering (~190 M), the lowering's
group-facts pass (~180 M), the parser's symbol-indexed binding array with a
LIFO undo stack (~150 M), the preprocessor's per-64-token chunk class masks

each), an inlined arena bump with the commit outlined (~90 M), the
lowering's O(1) slot maps (~90 M), the FAST prepass's prewarmed per-opcode
classification word (~60 M), and demand-driven type layout (~55 M). SPMD
codegen is the only wall-clock lever of its size and costs ~8 M instructions
to gain it.
