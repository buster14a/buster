**Stage 1 — translation chunk kernel (PR 804, merged; -55,10 M, -0,5036%).**
A plain newline copies through translation unchanged, so a 64-byte chunk
whose only stop bytes are newlines stores whole with one vector store;
line-start checkpoints, line and column advance from the newline mask; only
stream-editing bytes — `\r`, a splice-opening backslash, a backslash on the
last lane — fall to the exact scalar loop at their own byte.  The scalar
loop stays as fallback and reference: `force_scalar` threads into
translation, and the differential comparator now also checks translated
bytes, the checkpoint array, its offsets and the page brackets.
Instructions 10.941.041.538 -> 10.885.942.234 (-55.099.304), branches
-13.505.008 (-0,6729%).

**Stage 2 — line metrics by segment subtraction (PR 807; -5,21 M,
-0,0479%).**  Subtracting a segment's code or comment marks from the newline
mask borrows through exactly that segment (in-segment marks sum below the
segment's newline bit, so no borrow crosses it), and the cleared newlines
are the lines holding a mark; the five metrics counters advance by popcount.
A block comment's newline marks the next line through a one-bit shift of the
same subtraction; the carried state folds into the lowest newline; marks
above the last newline stay out and become the next window's carry.

**Stage 3 — vectorized checkpoint rows: MEASURED NEGATIVE, reverted, do not
retry as scoped (+4,03 M, +0,0370%, 9/9; branches -0,28 M).**  The 12-byte
`IrSourceCheckpoint` rows interleave exactly like the token emitter's
16-row stores (vpcompressb positions, three vpermi2d over offsets/lines,
column-one dwords from the store masks' complement), and the form works —
but the flat ~16-instruction emission loses to the ~9-instruction-per-
newline scalar loop at the real density of one to three newlines per chunk.
The mean chunk holds too few rows for a 16-row store to amortize; a
count-thresholded hybrid would buy back only the rare dense chunks while
adding a branch to all of them.

**Stage 4 — mixed-window cursor classes: MEASURED NEGATIVE, reverted, do
not retry (+8,57 M, +0,0787%, 9/9; branches +0,36 M).**  Two branchless
window classes replaced the serial cursor where the masks could prove them:
strings-only windows paired unescaped quotes by carry-less-multiply
prefix-xor (escape parity over the whole window, literal prefixes and
defer/unterminated edges in mask arithmetic, numbers by the carry formulas
on seeds outside spans), and line-comment-only windows resolved first-open-
per-line and span-to-newline with two bitstring subtractions.  Both passed
the whole differential gate — the port is correct — and the composite still
measured +8,57 M: roughly four times the entire residue it targets.  The
mechanism is the in-loop code-growth/register-pressure trap
(`feedback-perf-measurement-traps`): three extra `chunk0` compares and the
class state extend live ranges across the window body, and all 313 K
windows pay while at most the 57 K mixed windows could save.  The serial
cursor at ~3 M is the floor for this loop shape; any future attempt must
move the work *out* of the window loop, not add provable classes inside it.

**The overlap verdict, priced from the census, no build:** of 3,6 M
re-loaded lanes, 2,3 M are defer tails whose removal requires carrying lexer
state across fixed-stride windows — which would also move the 14,2 M bytes
that scan_one's 64-byte stride finds consume at ~10 instructions per chunk
into full window classification at ~10x that, decisively negative on this
corpus (48% of lexed bytes are long literals and comments).  The remaining
1,3 M boundary-tail lanes (mean 6,05 bytes on 215,8 K full-advance windows)
can only be rescued when the tail item provably closes at exactly the
window edge, which the tail-length distribution prices under 1 M.
Item-aligned windows with the scalar escape are the right architecture for
this corpus; the overlap lead is closed.
