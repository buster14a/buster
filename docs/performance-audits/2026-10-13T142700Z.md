## Scope

This audit closes the measured arms of
[#58](https://github.com/buster14a/buster/issues/58), which compares three
x86-64 register-load selectors in `machine_x64_select_load`: A (helper-based,
[a.patch](evidence/2026-09-30T191439Z/issue58/a.patch)), B (branched,
[b.patch](evidence/2026-09-30T191439Z/issue58/b.patch)) and C (production
branchless masks). [`2026-10-12T183752Z`](2026-10-12T183752Z.md) left three
parts open:

- a Zen 5 measurement of self-built A and B compilers under
  `-fcanonical-inline`;
- Zen 5 front-end PMU evidence;
- measured register pressure and spills.

Earlier audits are unchanged.

## Disposition

**Keep C, the production branchless masks.** Neither alternative improves
compiler time to an artifact on Zen 5:

- With Clang-built compilers, A and B are within ±0.11% of C.
- With self-built inline-mode compilers, A is unchanged and B is 0.13%
  slower.
- Front-end topdown does not separate either variant from C's own variation.
- Spill reduction is about 0.04%.

The #48 inliner cannot inline A's helpers (see
[`2026-10-12T183752Z`](2026-10-12T183752Z.md)), so a self-built A is a
helper-calling A. Its parity with C rules out a measurable cost from those
calls, not just a hidden gain. With this measurement, the open arms of #58
are complete. Performance-validation coverage is recorded by the two check
runs above. It covers `uarch_lab:compare`, `build:bench_throughput`,
`uarch_lab:compare@canonical-inline-self-host` and
`uarch_lab:compare@self-host-inline-compare`, and only for these exact
heads.
