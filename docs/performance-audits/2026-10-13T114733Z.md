## Method

Each workload ran from the repository root under
`valgrind --tool=callgrind --cache-sim=no --branch-sim=no` with the same
command line for both binaries. `ide cc` output was pinned with
`-march=znver3`. The inputs are `tests/differential/observables.c`, which the
differential harness links as a dynamic ELF about 3.7k times per CI run; a
`stdio.h` program with one constructor and one destructor; `int main(void)`,
which links as the static freestanding shape; a one-function and an empty
unit with `-c`; and `tests/basic_c_operations.c` with `-c`. Inclusive costs
come from `callgrind_annotate --auto=no --inclusive=yes`, read from this
checkout.

In the base, these consumers made 35 `buster_x86_metadata_encode` calls, and
the selector they reached cost 7,716,965 Ir. The stub and finalizer rows
overlap: the stub calls the finalizer registration. With #3796 merged, XOR's
candidate normalization had moved out of codegen and into the first linker
query, which is why `forwarding_prepare` cost 2.5 M rather than the 0.7 M
recorded in `2026-10-12T234403Z`. The candidate makes no
`buster_x86_metadata_encode` call in any of the six workloads. The constructor
link's larger saving is the same mechanism over more stub and PLT shapes.
The `-c` saving on `basic_c_operations.c` is the padding NOP alone.

**What remains.** The `observables.c` link still spends 2,968,524 Ir in
`buster_x86_metadata_select_form`, unchanged from the base: four shape-cache
resolutions from `machine_x64_metadata_shape_resolve`, for codegen shapes that
#3796 left to the selector because they depend on the input.

## Limits

- Counts come from `-march=x86-64-v3` builds, whose SIMD kernels run their
  fallbacks; the removed work is scalar selector and normalization code, so
  the deltas should carry over, but no native-build count was taken.
- The `-c` workloads that need no padding are unchanged by design; the
  saving is per linking process, plus one NOP preparation per padded compile.
- PE, Mach-O and thunk writers still select through `link_x86_emit`; they
  were not measured here.
