## `goto` nests: no algorithmic term found

**Input.** [`gen3.py`](evidence/2026-10-13T091421Z/gen3.py) `goto D`:
`for (; x > i%7;) { if (x == i) goto Li; Li: ;` at each of D levels.

**Time.** Best of 7, `ide cc -c`, base binary:

| D | wall | user | sys | minor faults | max RSS |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 4,000 | 0.12 s | 0.08 s | 0.04 s | 25,849 | — |
| 8,000 | 0.16 s | 0.10 s | 0.06 s | 26,568 | — |
| 16,000 | 0.35 s | 0.21 s | 0.13 s | 51,435 | 175 MB |
| 24,000 | 0.65 s | 0.44 s | 0.20 s | 76,652 | 248 MB |
| 32,000 | 0.76 s | 0.56 s | 0.19 s | 99,432 | 321 MB |

That is 2.2× wall per doubling at 16k to 32k. The earlier "2.3× per doubling,
1.2–1.8 s at 32k" on #2676 came from single runs on a busier VM. The same
VM gives 2.3× for declaration nests and 1.9× for braceless `for`. RSS was
read as the maximum over the run so far, so only the rising rows count.

**Exact work.** The allocation-diagnostic base binary emits
`-fsource-metrics` counters
([`goto-counters.txt`](evidence/2026-10-13T091421Z/goto-counters.txt)).
Every work counter doubles exactly with D:

| D | body-scope stores | call-arity candidates | statement-extent spans | peels |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 8,000 | 176,007 | 16,000 | 8,000 | 0 |
| 16,000 | 352,007 | 32,000 | 16,000 | 0 |
| 32,000 | 704,007 | 64,000 | 32,000 | 0 |

Two counters grow faster than linearly: `c_census.parse.string_equal_bytes`
(72,012 → 264,012) and `c_census.semantic.string_equal_bytes` (181,917 →
597,113). Both stay under 1 MB at 32k and cannot account for the time.
`work.rederive.body_scope_share_tokens` is the hypothetical cost of the old
nested refill, not work done.

**Instructions.** Callgrind on the x86-64-v3 base build counts 264.7 M, 539.8
M and 949.2 M instructions at D = 2,000, 4,000 and 8,000, which is linear.
Valgrind cannot decode the native AVX-512 build. At D ≥ 16,000 the
x86-64-v3 build under valgrind is refused with `codegen.capacity
machine-scratch`, because valgrind limits the address space. The same binary
compiles D = 32,000 natively.

**Where the time goes.** gdb attach-sampling over 12 runs at each depth:

| Phase | samples per run, 16k | samples per run, 32k |
|---|---|---|
| lowering | 1.0 | 2.8 |
| analysis | 1.0 | 1.0 |
| instruction selection | 0.67 | 1.25 |
| `ir_prepare` | 0.08 | 1.17 |
| fast register allocation | 0.33 | 0.75 |

The `ir_prepare` samples are mostly `ir_cfg_publish_instruction_rows`: one
cycle-following permutation over the instruction array and one walk per
block, both linear. No frame dominates.

**Conclusion.** The work counters are exact and linear, the instruction
counts are linear as far as they can be measured, and no function
dominates. The residual is consistent with the working set leaving the
caches: RSS grows from 175 MB to 321 MB, and the permutation is
random-access. That is a hypothesis; this host has no counters to confirm it.
No code change is made for it. `frontend-nesting-v1` times the `goto` family
on the 9700X.

## Braceless `switch (x) case 0:` nests (fixed)

The nesting suite's `braceless-switch` family exposed a quadratic term that
#3802's family missed. That family nested `switch (x)` without a label.
`c_parse_statement_end` peeled `name:` labels but read `case c: S` as a
simple statement up to the next depth-zero `;`, which is the end of the
whole nest.

| D | base `-fsyntax-only` | base `-c` | candidate `-fsyntax-only` | candidate `-c` |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2,000 | 0.11 s | — | 0.009 s | 0.027 s |
| 4,000 | 0.38 s | — | — | — |
| 8,000 | 1.42 s | — | 0.022 s | 0.14 s |
| 16,000 | — | — | 0.034 s | 0.13 s |
| 32,000 | 25.80 s | 26.46 s | 0.065 s | 0.25 s |

The input is SHA-256 `c1b16b2b…9e46` at D = 32,000.

**Negative control.** The candidate with the peel disabled fails both new
tests in `c_frontend_tests`:
- Statement-end tokens for the family grow from 281,000 to 4,484,000 at
  depths 200 and 800, against a bound of five times the shallow count plus
  the depth.
- `braceless-case-bodies` is refused as `unsupported C function-body
  statement or expression near 'continue'` in all four allocator and
  frontend modes.

**Behaviour.** The base binary refused valid C such as
`for (; n < 5; n++) switch (x) case 0: if (n == 2) break; else continue;`.
The candidate compiles it, and the runtime results of 12 such programs
match gcc.

**Output identity.** For every `tests/**/*.c` (423 files), the 17 edge
programs and the D = 4 nesting sources, the base and candidate produced
identical exit status, diagnostics and objects. The exceptions are the two
programs the base refused, which the candidate compiles: `total=451
same=449 diff=2`.

## Hosted cost of `frontend-nesting-v1`

The candidate's `tools/benchmark_frontend_scaling.py --suite nesting
--warmups 1 --repetitions 11 --cpu 2` compared the candidate binary with a
copy of itself. It wrote complete provenance and 68 cells in 190 s, with
196 KB of JSON
([`hosted-nesting-results.json`](evidence/2026-10-13T091421Z/hosted-nesting-results.json),
[`hosted-nesting-provenance.json`](evidence/2026-10-13T091421Z/hosted-nesting-provenance.json)).
Callgrind and the test suite were running at the same time, so the time is
an upper bound. Across the 8k-to-32k step (4× the depth), the `-c` medians
grow 1.65× to 4.91× by family; the largest is `goto`. That is far below the
16× a quadratic term gives.
