## Observations

- **`-fsyntax-only` peak RSS does not move**, although the tree retains
  36.25 MB. Under `-c`, peak RSS grows by 31.7 MB, about 87% of the retained
  bytes. A likely explanation, not verified: the syntax-only peak is set by
  an earlier phase that is larger than the tree, while the `-c` peak comes
  later, when the tree is still alive alongside lowering and codegen.
- **The `-c` saving is mostly the frontend saving carried through.** The
  pilot saves 1.346 G instructions on `-fsyntax-only` and 1.375 G on `-c`.
  The wall-time savings are 56.5 ms and 66.5 ms.
- **The 9700X shows a larger gain than the hosted census.** Audit
  `2026-10-10T175428Z` measured about 8% fewer instructions with Callgrind on
  `-march=x86-64-v3`, on an earlier main. That census is diagnostic and was
  taken on a different build, flags and revision, so the gap is not a
  like-for-like delta.
- **The ordinary comparison in the same run was an A/A run.** The base and
  head binaries have the same sha256. It found no detectable difference:
  wall B/A 1.0003 [0.9999, 1.0005] over 194 pairs, 2.2589e10 instructions in
  both. The `throughput-corpus-v2` corpus had 12 cases, with 0 confirmed
  regressions and 0 inconclusive.
