## Profile and bounded change

A separate `cycles:u` capture retained 4,101 samples and zero lost samples.
Inclusive attribution was 54.47% under `c_analyze_with_options`, with 27.12%
under semantic-core work and 27.10% under lowering. Code generation was 23.15%.
These are one capture's overlapping call-tree shares, not additive wall-time
measurements or a forecast of a speedup. The diagnostic phase record is retained
separately from uninstrumented timing.

The candidate replaces token-backed `sizeof`, `_Alignof`, `__alignof` and
`__alignof__` spelling predicates with their existing well-known symbol IDs.
The expression leaf retains its distinct accepted set, and plain C23 `alignof`
remains a separate rule. It adds no names, token fields, analysis pass or cache.
Uninterned identifier and unclassified test tokens retain spelling lookup.

The extended token-kind oracle exposed a helper edge case: the old
`c_token_in_well_known_set` recovered an oversized spelling before rejecting an
uninterned nonword token. A no-spelling row faulted in the new test. The final
candidate performs the kind check first. This is an internal predicate boundary
finding, not evidence of a source-input crash; the reproduction and superseded
candidate are recorded on the issue.
