## Census: which shapes still reached the selector

A disposable probe (not committed) printed every registration that
`machine_x64_metadata_shape_resolve` resolved through
`buster_x86_metadata_select_form`, and dumped the selector's key for all 344
registrations.

- 330 of the 359 work-ledger fixtures resolved at least one shape through
  the selector, 2,872 times in total and up to 56 times in one unit, over
  145 distinct shapes. The self-host input resolved 56. Together they reached
  150 of the 275 distinct shapes.
- The other 125 are the six #3796 already pinned, and shapes this corpus
  does not emit: mostly integer moves and arithmetic at other widths and
  displacement or immediate classes (23 `MOV`, 11 `LEA`, 7–9 each of `SUB`,
  `AND`, `CMP` and `ADD`), plus AVX-512 predicate and vector moves. Which of
  them a compile reaches depends on its input, and a hand-picked subset
  would have left that tail on the selector. Every shape is therefore
  pinned, from one table (`machine_x64_metadata_shape_pins`, 275 rows of 16
  bytes) rather than per-call-site keys.
- Each of the 69 duplicate registrations selects the same form as the first
  registration of its signature, so duplicates share their entry's pin.
- The low 32 bits of the shape signature take only 213 distinct values over
  the 275 shapes: the mix carries bits mostly upward, and many operand
  fields sit above bit 31. The signature folded to 32 bits
  (`signature ^ signature >> 32`) is distinct for all 275, so the folded
  value tags each row.

## Method

Each workload ran from the repository root under
`valgrind --tool=callgrind --cache-sim=no --branch-sim=no` with the same
command line for both binaries. `ide cc` output was pinned with
`-march=znver3`. The inputs are those of `2026-10-13T114733Z` plus
`tests/c_abi_cfuncs.c`: `tests/basic_c_operations.c` with `-c`; a dynamic ELF
link of `tests/differential/observables.c`; a `stdio.h` program with one
constructor and one destructor; `int main(void)` linked; the same unit and an
empty unit with `-c`; and `tests/c_abi_cfuncs.c` (560 KB) with `-c`.
Inclusive costs come from `callgrind_annotate --auto=no --inclusive=yes`.

| empty `-c` | 10,320,122 | 10,327,790 | +7,668 (+0.07%) |

Every output was byte-identical between the two binaries.

**Where it went.** `buster_x86_metadata_select_form`, inclusive, in the base:
9,601,508 Ir in the `basic_c_operations.c` compile (18.9%), 2,978,868 in the
`observables.c` link, 2,525,714 in the constructor link and 6,113,966 in
`c_abi_cfuncs.c`. The candidate reaches the selector in none of the seven
workloads. A pinned shape's first lookup still prepares its exact plan, so
the saving is the selector's search and the normalization of the candidate
forms it rejects.

**What it costs.** Attaching a pin to each of the 344 registrations adds
about 22 Ir per registration to the shape-cache prewarm (+8.6 k self in
`machine_x64_metadata_shape_cache_add` and its caller, net of inlining
changes). The empty, one-function and `int main(void)` workloads, which
reached at most one selected shape in the base, pay +7.5–7.7 k. No other
function in them changes by more than 1 k, so the forms that lone selection
normalized are evidently normalized by a later consumer in the candidate.

- **Work-ledger corpus.** 360 units each: the 359 fixture objects and the
  linked self-host `ide`. Ledger and plain artifacts agree within both
  subjects; "Plain artifacts byte-identical to the baseline: 360/360".
- **Linked executables.** Every `tests/*.c` and `tests/differential/*.c`

  `-march=znver3`, no flags, and `-g -O2 -march=znver3`. Of 260–265 sources

  exit status, stdout and diagnostics match.
- **Objects.** The same 421 sources with `-c`, in four legs: no flags,
  `-fregister-allocator=fast -v`, `-fregister-allocator=quality -v`, and
  `-g -fno-machine-fallback`. 383 compile in each leg; all objects, `-v`
  statistics, diagnostics and exit statuses match.

## Stale pins

Two deliberately corrupted builds, never committed:

- A wrong `stable_hash` on the `SUB r64, imm8` row refuses the compiles that
  reach that shape with `codegen.unsupported-instruction`, while a
  one-function unit that does not reach it compiles. The machine test then
  fails with `1 stale rows in machine_x64_metadata_shape_pins; the selector
  asks for:` and the 275 rows, which match the committed table byte for
  byte.
- A wrong signature tag on one `AND` row refuses every x86-64 compile;
  AArch64 compiles are unaffected.

## Limits

- Counts come from `-march=x86-64-v3` builds, whose SIMD kernels run their
  fallbacks; the removed work is scalar selector and normalization code, so
  the deltas should carry over, but no native-build count was taken.
- The PE and Mach-O startup writers in `link.c`, and the source assembler,
  still select; they were not measured here.
