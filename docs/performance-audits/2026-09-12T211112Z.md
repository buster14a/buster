Issue [#120](https://github.com/buster14a/buster/issues/120). The candidate
replaces twelve line segments per quadratic with bounded device-space
subdivision at 0.25 pixel error. This is a local laptop measurement of the
complete bitmap call, not a dedicated-host performance acceptance result.

## Corpus and method

The retained [benchmark source](evidence/2026-09-12T211112Z/truetype_benchmark.c)
includes the repository's `truetype.c` directly and supplies a precommitted
256 MiB arena so both variants exercise the same parser, outline builder and
active-edge rasterizer without OS allocation noise. The baseline is
`9666ce1c468b8a2484e47bb6a71dce366c6a40e9`; the candidate is the working
change based on `7efd6c7ef203cc2ff590c8cb233ee50d335474ba` before its final rebase.
No TrueType source changed between those two main revisions.

Five Noto fonts cover Latin sans, Latin serif, Arabic, Devanagari and symbols.
Each contributes 64 consecutive codepoints from its representative block, for
320 requested glyphs per scale. The font SHA-256 identities are:

| Font | SHA-256 |
|---|---|
| Noto Sans Regular | `478c558ea716033cd60c03438f628dfa75694dcf6b5f6d505a2f05fd2b4f3823` |
| Noto Serif Regular | `19e72cd8d595fae5bd74a5206f5d938512e1183d4fed7abb1ec1be1d7efa5f88` |
| Noto Sans Arabic Regular | `bdff3e5659d67e67def05b33f749683b9376ae819d65d3dd62ac4640b3aaef48` |
| Noto Sans Devanagari Regular | `306b53ecfb182a504dd8a7446093c316387d2fd8dc350d0792ed1753fe0996cd` |
| Noto Sans Symbols Regular | `d0e98e9a2c046594c5021437273943be7e79e0fd980fde125279e22302212595` |

For each font and requested pixel height, `truetype_scale_for_pixel_height`
selects the scale. Segment counts come from one outline extraction. Timing is
nine complete 320-glyph rounds after two warmups; the table reports the median
of three process-level medians. Variant order was
baseline/candidate/candidate/baseline/baseline/candidate, pinned to CPU 0.
Each bitmap contributes a sampled pixel to a retained digest. The differing
digests are expected because the candidate changes curve geometry.

```sh
clang -O3 -DNDEBUG -DBUSTER_OPTIMIZE=1 -fwrapv -fno-strict-aliasing \
  -funsigned-char -ffunction-sections -fdata-sections -I<source>/src \
  docs/performance-audits/evidence/2026-09-12T211112Z/truetype_benchmark.c \
  -Wl,--gc-sections -lm -o <binary>
taskset -c 0 <binary>
```

## Results

| Pixel height | Fixed segments | Adaptive segments | Segment reduction | Fixed median | Adaptive median | Time reduction |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 12 | 60,857 | 7,869 | 87.1% | 25.629 ms | 13.733 ms | 46.4% |
| 32 | 60,857 | 8,698 | 85.7% | 51.811 ms | 35.176 ms | 32.1% |
| 128 | 60,857 | 12,983 | 78.7% | 214.735 ms | 170.671 ms | 20.5% |

Adaptive output grows with device scale while remaining far below the fixed
twelve-segment policy on this corpus. The time direction agrees at all three
sizes despite the candidate's deliberate count-then-emit parse. Absolute time
is noisy on this non-dedicated host; the segment counts are deterministic.
