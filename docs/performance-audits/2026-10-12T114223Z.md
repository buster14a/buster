## Observed traversal populations

The new standalone C probe drives the actual splitter; the
[coverage collector](../../tools/machine_edge_split_scaling_coverage.py)
extracts LLVM execution-region counts from its instrumented binary. The
[method and complete build command](../agents/benchmarking.md#parameter-edge-splitting-populations)
use Clang/LLVM 23.1.1, `-O2 -g`, the repository's three C semantic flags,
single-threading and section garbage collection. Coverage is separate from
the trusted whole-compiler binary and is not a timing build.

All 56 cells pass: x86-64 and AArch64 MIR, chain/fanout/duplicate/no-split
shapes, and 0/1/2/17/257/4097/16384 blocks. Reversed raw edges expose stable
ordering. Each duplicate destination has an earlier non-copy edge and two
copy edges; only the first splitting edge may win. Whole generated block,
instruction, edge and switch records are compared with closed-form expected
records. Copy offsets survive. These are structural fixtures with synthetic
copy slices, not independently executable programs or allocator inputs.

For every splitting cell, the initial split count, histogram construction,
CSR scatter, split numbering, destination-map population, sparse reset and
edge publication each visit exactly E entries. The prefix visits B blocks;
instruction copying visits I rows; the split predicate is called 2E times.
The remapping helper is called Q times (2(B-1) for chains, B for fanout and
duplicates), and its source is a constant-time pair of indexed lookups.
These are observed counts of the named traversals, not a substitute for
whole-compiler instructions or a proof about every uncounted operation.

No-split cells perform only the original E-entry predicate/count walk. All
indexing, copying, publication and remap counters are zero, the complete
function record is unchanged, and allocation/high-water growth are zero.
Empty and single-block controls have zero visits throughout.

[counts.json](evidence/2026-10-12T114223Z-edge-split/counts.json) records all
cells, selected raw LLVM regions, source coordinates, profile digests,
binary/source/probe/collector hashes and checked output hashes. Full raw LLVM
profiles are retained in the session's evidence bundle; the committed record
is their compact projection. The collector rejects a missing profile or count
mismatch rather than filling it from the expected formula. A negative run with
the uninstrumented binary fails at profile merge, as required.

The scoped arena high water measures the production call's temporary peak;
the retained cursor includes only its output arrays. Observed peak-minus-retained
bytes equal `4 * (I + 2E + 3B + 1)` in these cells, with no alignment slack.
The checked bound permits up to 64 bytes for alignment. Fixture construction
and checking are outside the scope and timer.

The uninstrumented Clang probe passes two repetitions of every cell (112
records in [controls.txt](evidence/2026-10-12T114223Z-edge-split/controls.txt)).
