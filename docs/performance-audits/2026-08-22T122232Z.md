**Lead 1 — the 4-byte `Token` to 2 bytes — is dead: its population no longer
exists.**  The record was the *buster tokenizer's* token (u32 id:8/length:24,
the `2026-08-09c` Windows-ABI fix), a bench-side lead for the custom-language
frontend that was removed outright in the 2026-08-14 consolidation; nothing
in the tree lexes into a 4-byte row today.  The C-side successor question —
`CToken` below its current 12 bytes — was sized and is adjudicated against
rather than untried: stage 1 creates 3.165.160 lexed rows (38,0 MB at 12 B)
and materializes 2.937.382 into the final stream (35,2 MB), and the shrink
ladder's own history prices the next rung.  48 -> 16 (`2026-08-09g`) paid
+2,2% instructions for -16,3% faults; 16 -> 12 (`2026-08-16e`) paid +1,49%
instructions for -4,1% L1d misses and was already called the weaker exchange.
Reaching 8 forces out one of two load-bearing u32s: `symbol`, which would
reintroduce per-use spelling hashing — the exact cost `2026-08-08k` proposal
2 removed and **this audit's other half removes more of** — or `offset`,
which is the token's spelling and location identity and is required by the
parse side's random access (`2026-08-08k` itself: "the C parse side still
needs random token access ... the buster side alone can go lengths-only",
and the buster side is gone).  A parallel side array moves the bytes without
shrinking them.  No configuration of this lead has a payer; do not retry it
as spelled.

**Lead 2 — the keyword hash — re-derived into today's shape and taken.**
The recorded lead ("vectorized keyword hash") targeted the removed
tokenizer's perfect hash; the C frontend meanwhile grew the better mechanism
— identifiers intern once, keywords sit in the predefined id range,
`class_bits` answers the declaration-keyword class per id, and the
token-class cache over it measured at its floor here (401 spelling computes
out of 1.448.838 queries; `c_symbol_intern` itself is floor-measured in
`2026-08-22f` and untouched).  What remained was the *specifier-word* family
still answering by spelling.  Probe counters over one stage-1 compile (the
`2026-08-22b` harness) sized the population:

    c_parse_type_word_for_dialect      363.733 calls   length-switch, <=9 memcmps, 2,9 MB compared
    c_parse_type_qualifier_word        355.356 calls   <=13 memcmps
    c_parse_auto_type_word             343.327 calls   1 memcmp
    c_parse_atomic_declaration_prefix  154.961 calls   <=13 memcmps after the qualifier miss
    c_declaration_keyword (FNV+probe)   63.371 calls   820.780 spelling bytes hashed
    c_parse_statement_keyword_at             6 calls   dead, left alone
    symbol==0 at the hottest site           99 calls   the fallback is real but rare

~1,34 M calls per compile, every word already interned predefined.  The fix
is the `c_token_in_well_known_set` contract extended to this family:
`CSymbolTable` gains `word_bits` (u16 per predefined id, 512 B, derived at
create time from the same spelling predicates the symbol-0 fallback still
runs, so the paths cannot drift), `_token` variants answer with one load and
one mask, and the token-bearing call sites — the specifier-prefix scans, the
qualified-typedef loops, the declarator name scans, `c_parse_type_start`,
and the aggregate-introducer scans (via a new `C_PARSE_AGGREGATE_KEYWORDS`
well-known set) — consume them.  `__int128` joins the classified extras as
the one `c_parse_type_word` spelling not already predefined, which is what
makes `symbol > predefined_limit` a constant-time "no".
