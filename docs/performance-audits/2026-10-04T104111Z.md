## The three architectural assumptions

### A1. No AST: every phase re-walks token ranges; facts are discarded at the validate-to-lower boundary

`c_parse_ast` "builds no tree; every later consumer re-walks token ranges"
(`c_parse.c` orientation header). `c_parse_validate_lowering_constraints`
runs, per function body, about sixteen validation families
(`c_parse_validate_generic_duplicates` ... `c_parse_validate_statement_expressions`),
each rescanning candidate tokens, re-entering scopes through
`c_parse_scope_for_token`/`c_parse_body_scopes_build`, and typing its
constructs through the speculative `CTypeParseMachine`
(`c_parse_expression_type_query`: one 488-byte `CParseResult` checkpoint and
400-byte frame rows per uncached query). Every result is released at the end
of the body (`arena_set_position(machine->scratch_arena, validation_mark)`;
`result->expression_scalar_types = 0`). Lowering (`c_gen.c`: "There is no AST
-- lowering re-walks token ranges directly") then re-derives expression
structure with three further typers: the depth-bounded recursive precedence
splitter `c_ir_sizeof_operand_type_attempt_depth`, the heuristic
`c_ir_predict_expression_type` layered on it (its comments record repairs for
cJSON, Lua, zlib, glibc and musl inputs), and bottom-up canonical typing
(`c_ir_usual_arithmetic_type`), which needed C-only fields on `IrType`
(`integer_conversion_rank`, `is_complex`, `is_transparent_union`,
`is_unprototyped`) because C typing is redone in the canonical type domain.

Retain: tokens are 12 bytes; a node tree for 3.76 million tokens is on the
order of 100 MB of fresh pages while `clear_page_erms` already dominates kernel
samples; token-indexed sidecars (`identifier_use_by_token_plus_one`,
`CNumberFacts`, the string-literal memo, `CTokenPositionIndex`,
`CArrayBound.inferred_count`) already carry the facts found worth carrying.
Replace: the one missing sidecar, "type per expression", is what forces the
family re-walks, the speculative typer, the four lowering typers, and (A2) the
unknown per-body type closure. Falsifier for retain: the pass's cost is
mostly representation-induced and separable from its checks (tested below).
Falsifier for replace: a retained typed sidecar touches more pages than it
removes work; the pass touches 63 MB of its 7.18 GB of arena requests, so a
sidecar must be comparably sparse.

### A2. The translation unit is the unit of state

One `CIntegerIrBuilder`, one arena, module-wide dense row streams
(`CIrRowStreams`), 79 on-demand `c_ir_add_pointer_type(builder->program, ...)`
sites and lazily published runtime-call symbols found by a linear
`string_equal` scan of the symbol table (`c_ir_emit_runtime_call_source`).
Canonical ids are function-local (`IrFunction` owns its arrays), which favors
per-function work; the blocker is the lazy shared publication that #531's own
census found. Replayed Amdahl bound from the 2026-09-23 sample: phases serial
by construction (preprocess 4.26 + parse 1.73 + semantic 29.49 + lowering
25.20 + object 2.04 + other 0.69) are **63.4%** of user cycles. With codegen
(25.13%) and preparation (11.45%) perfectly parallel on eight threads the
single-TU self-compile reaches at most **1.47x** (1.52x on sixteen). The type
closure a body needs is discoverable only by typing it, which the validation
pass already does and discards; A1 and A2 share one missing artifact.

### A3. No input-dependent recursion and no function-pointer dispatch, applied to the frontend

Every speculative token re-parse becomes a materialized machine: 400-byte
`CTypeParseFrame` rows, a 488-byte whole-result checkpoint per uncached query,
rollback by snapshot (`c_type_parse_rollback`), and switch dispatch over 41
frame kinds in the lowering/query machines with suspension and resume. The
lowering already breaks the letter of the rule with a depth-bounded recursive
typer. Measured below: the pass copies 329.7 MB of checkpoints and 506.2 MB of
frames on the self-compile and rolls back 99,291 of 675,644 uncached queries,
so 85% of checkpoints are never used; #1532 and #1533 recovered -1.7% and
-0.7% Ir against this family. Retain: stack safety for a self-hosting compiler
and for sanitizer/fuzz builds, no indirect calls. Replace: a depth-guarded
recursive typer gives the same safety, and a non-speculative typer needs no
snapshots, but only once there is a tree to type.

## Verdict

1. On valid input the pass produces nothing the code generator consumes. Its
   only consumer-visible effect is **+429,675 bytes (+20.8%) of `.debug_info`**
   from types interned during speculative typing and the scalar prepublication
   at `c_parse.c:27894`, the `.debug_info` sensitivity #1028 recorded, now sized.
2. Of the 17-19%, roughly five points are work any compiler does once (type
   each expression, fold each constant, lay out each type); the remaining
   12-14 points are the family re-walk, speculative machine overhead,
   duplicate initializer folding, whole-table layout seeding, eager diagnostic
   work and the discard itself. The ceiling is structural (A1), not a matter of
   more cache cuts: #1532, #1533 and #1538 recovered about 3.7% Ir combined
   against this pool, and lowering-side reuse is capped at the 1.18% Ir of
   `c_ir_query_execute`.
3. The pass cannot simply be dropped: canonical IR erases `const` (`IrType`
   carries only `is_atomic` and `is_volatile`), so lowering cannot detect
   const assignment, return/arity incompatibility or duplicate cases.
   "Lower first, validate on failure" would silently accept invalid programs
   and is **rejected**.
4. Smallest reversible intervention (hypothesis, not implemented): migrate
   one family at a time from the eager per-body scan to the lowering site that
   already holds the C entity or member, starting with
   `c_parse_validate_const_assignments` (5.94% inclusive) at
   `c_ir_assignment_expression_place_frame_push` /
   `c_ir_emit_field_place_from_value`, behind a flag; keep the eager family
   for `-fsyntax-only`; make the location at `c_parse.c:6243` lazy. Reject the
   move if any negative fixture changes its diagnostic text or location, if
   the 397-unit work-ledger corpus is not byte-identical at `-g0`, if the `-g`
   `.debug_info` reduction drops a DIE a debugger needs (compare DIE sets, not
   sizes), if the admitted 9700X harness shows under about 5% wall gain for
   the first family, or if the moved check needs a C fact lowering does not
   already hold. Whether the qualifier is reachable at those exact sites is
   unverified.
