## Validation and limits

- Callgrind counts instructions, not time, on `-march=x86-64-v3` builds whose
  AVX-512 kernels are compiled out.
- One input, one host, single process, no pinning and no `perf` counters.
  Wall time was not measured.
- No PR benchmark request is made for this slice, and the 9700X comparison
  cannot time the hook. Zen 5 validation stays **incomplete** (#2761).
- The mutation check made `c_parse_plan_append_braced` emit one unsplit
  segment. The segment binder then reads over the braces without the scope the
  loop gives a `{`. `c_ast_test_statements` failed in every layout (35 scopes
  without the tree, 22 with it), and so did the corpus differential on 22
  fixtures.
- The rest of the candidate's correctness record (Debug, the self-host fixed
  point, the analyzer, `source_size`) is in the pull request.
