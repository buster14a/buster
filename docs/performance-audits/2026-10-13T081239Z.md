## The one design and its result

Reserve one callee-saved general register for the eligible immutable escaping
value with the most cross-block uses, requiring at least three. The register
must be untouched by explicit physical operands and declared clobbers across
the whole function. Reuse the existing pin machinery, with whole-function
ownership to cover every loop and path; no baseline allocation replay, sorting,
splits or acceptance retries. Exclude constrained/mutable/rematerializable
values, block parameters without ordinary definitions, and ABI captures.
Thus this does not adopt the owner's deferred slice B. QUALITY is unchanged.

Screen declared before measurement: at least 5% fewer combined boundary
spills+reloads on **each** FAST target, no increased total spills+reloads or code
bytes, applicable correctness gates, and a compile-CPU 95% upper bound below
+1%. A static/cost failure rejects this design without retuning. Passing would
still require owner-requested 9700X generated-runtime evidence.
