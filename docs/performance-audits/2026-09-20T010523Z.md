## Experiment contract

Exact submitted heads are compared with their main-branch merge bases, not
with a later main containing unrelated compiler changes. All variants consume
the frozen `82a63c5110aa63c3fb0e03d045a49343148ca787` source tree and its
generated headers. The current local checkout and its unrelated modified
`tools/native_retirement_census.c` are not benchmark inputs.

The dedicated host is reached through `tailscale ssh david@benchpress`.
Fresh detached worktrees are configured with `./build.sh generate` and built
with `./build.sh build --config Release -t ide`, using the canonical TCC
bootstrap and trusted Clang Release producer. Builds and measurements are
sequential under `/home/david/bench/host.lock`. CPU 2 runs every measured
compile. No hardware-counter collection is inside the timed interval.

The schedule is fixed before seeing results: A/A followed by #853, #854,
#856 and #857; two warmups per leg, then two rounds of 20 adjacent pairs
per comparison. Each two-pair block contains AB and BA; seed 791 determines
the first orientation. No observations are discarded. Monotonic wall time
covers process launch through completion. Every executable hash and every
`IR_FAST`, `IR_LOCAL_PROMOTION`, `IR_LOCAL_PROMOTION_WORK` and `CODEGEN`
summary must agree within every A/B comparison.

For each round, report the median of ten AB/BA block geometric-mean
candidate/baseline ratios. The minimum and maximum of those ten block ratios
form a conservative distribution-free interval for the population median:
coverage is at least `1 - 2 / 2^10 = 99.8046875%` for independent blocks.
Across all ten rounds (including A/A), the union bound gives at least
98.046875% simultaneous coverage under that assumption. Host drift and
serial correlation can violate the independence assumption. A/A is a noise
diagnostic, not a guarantee of host isolation. Both candidate rounds must
exclude no-change in the favorable direction before claiming a repeatable
benefit on this workload. A result spanning no-change is inconclusive, not
equivalence. This focused diagnostic does not implement the separate native
retirement acceptance contract or establish a general workload speedup.

## Execution correction

The first runner incorrectly required byte equality across different main
baselines as well as within each comparison. It stopped at the first #854
baseline warmup: the `e013ef03` compiler produced different executable bytes
from the `82a63c5` compiler on the frozen input, although their recorded
compiler summaries were identical. Those baseline revisions contain unrelated
frontend changes. No #854 timed sample had been collected.

The remaining-case runner resets the expected artifact at each comparison;
it still requires byte-identical baseline/candidate outputs and identical
summaries for every warmup and timed compile of that comparison. Completed
A/A and #853 samples were retained unchanged and were not rerun. The failed
cross-case check and original runner log are retained on the remote host.
