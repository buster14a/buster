## Why slots end up fixed

A second uncommitted probe (`slot-probe.py`) classified every slot of every
function that coalesces slots, at `-g0`:

- **static**: volatile, or the outgoing argument area;
- **escaping**: the address escapes, per `machine_fast_certify_frame_addresses`;
- **unproven**: the census found a touch it cannot account for;
- **untouched**;
- **shareable**.

| Workload | Functions | Slots | Static | Escaping | Unproven | Untouched | Shareable |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| SQLite | 727 | 1,665 | 4 | 1,010 | 0 | 0 | 651 |
| self-host unit | 5,034 | 145,598 | 12 | 17,820 | 8,455 | 155 | 119,156 |

In the 396 SQLite functions that share nothing, the slots are 612 escaping and
3 static. A purely static pre-filter, decided before any row walk, would skip
only 1 function per workload, so it is not worth its code.

The escaping slots are known once certification ends, which is before the
touch census runs. The census only widens ranges and fixes slots. Nothing after
it reads those results unless some slot is still unfixed: the closure closes
zero objects and the colorer is not called. So when certification and the
static rules already fix every slot, the census is skipped. That covers all 396
such SQLite functions (71,761 rows) and 143 self-host functions at `-g0`
(16,450 rows). Stage-1 self-host (`-g`) is unaffected, because `-g` already
turns slot coalescing off.

## Effect

This is the slot certification-plus-census phase on SQLite, in median probe
cycles (`census-phase-sqlite.txt`):

| Population | Functions | Rows | Baseline | Candidate |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| no slots | 1,893 | 111,187 | 0.31 M | 0.33 M |
| slots, none shareable (skipped) | 396 | 71,761 | 6.20 M | 3.93 M |
| some slot shareable | 331 | 129,076 | 9.87 M | 10.38 M |

The skipped population falls by 37%. Certification still runs for it. The
other populations stay within this host's run-to-run noise.

Whole-placement totals moved about +6% in every population, including the ones
the change cannot reach. That is drift between the two builds and on the host,
not a measurement of this change, so no whole-compile number is claimed.

## Correctness

The baseline `ide` (SHA-256 `43a39a91…ca07012a`) and the candidate `ide`
(`c10198be…a1defec3ff`) compiled the same candidate source and SQLite input.
All six objects are byte-identical:

- SQLite at `-g` and `-g0`;
- the self-host unit at `-g` and `-g0`, with `-march=native` and with the
  default ISA.

The gates are listed in the pull request.

This composes with #3792, which caches the census touches for the closure.
With every slot fixed, the closure never reads the census, so whichever change
lands second takes only a conflict on the census loop's condition.
