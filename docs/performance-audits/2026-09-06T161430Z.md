## What was re-priced, and what that changed

**The `#embed` route for the x86 metadata tables was ranked first among what
was left, at ~350 M with a reader's original estimate of ~1.100 M. It is worth
at most 90 M on this tree, and should not be built.** Measured directly rather
than estimated: compiling `x86_64-assembly.generated.h` — 6,05 MB, of which
5,8 MB is string literals spread over 1.378 lines — costs **89,7 M**
instructions (203,65 M for a translation unit including it against 113,91 M
for the same unit without it). That figure already contains the emission of
5,99 MB of `.rodata`, which `#embed` does not remove, so its realistic ceiling
is 60-75 M. The reason is that the previous round's 64-byte string-literal
decode kernel and its initializer leaf-class folder already took roughly 85%
of this item. It needed `#embed` support in the frontend and a build step that
bakes the tables; that price has not changed and the prize has collapsed.

**The range-based macro-expansion engine, ranked second at ~280 M and "weeks",
is re-priced at 45-65 M integrated and is not recommended.** Its estimate
predates four of its own cost drivers landing: `CPpToken` shrunk 36 -> 16 bytes
with a per-line stamp table, the by-symbol macro table, and child contexts for
arguments naming no macro, which are now **918 of 47.955**. The whole expansion
machinery measures ~266 M here (`c_preprocess_expand` 117 M,
`c_macro_materialize` 71 M, `process_expanded_line` ~72 M, continuation
advance 6 M), and a range engine cannot remove the emit store, the
foreign-spelling copy, the substitution itself or the stamps. What is genuinely
range-shaped is the 2,93 M node allocations, the two pointer walks of the
output list and moving pass-through runs — ceiling 90-110 M standalone, and
45-65 M integrated because the inlined arena bump below already takes most of
the allocation half. The cheap subset — one shared LIFO task array per
`c_preprocess_expand`, the nesting having been checked to be strictly LIFO —
is worth ~25-40 M for about a day, and the two negatives recorded below say it
must be a *pure removal* of work, never a trade.

**The lowering's group-facts pass, ranked at ~180 M, is now a much smaller
prize** — the two items that landed (the matching-delimiter hoist and the lazy
operand-scan class byte) took most of the walks it was meant to replace. What
remains of it is four group-fact walkers running 4,93 M chained-`else if`
iterations, worth ~25 M as a class-byte table.

**The parser's symbol-indexed binding array, ranked at ~150 M, measured
-25,58 M**, and the FAST allocator's `contract_*`/`out_*` fills, ranked at
~250 M, measured **-4,64 M**. Both were real and both landed; both were
sample-share estimates of items whose instruction cost was much smaller than
their cycle cost.

**`c_parse_lookup_entity_symbol` is not cache-bound.** It carries 3,5x more
cycles than instructions, which reads like a memory stall, but its L1d miss
share is **0,64x** its cycle share. The stall is the dependent pointer chase
plus a mispredicting compare, not footprint — which is why the binding array
that replaces the chase with one indexed load was the right fix and why its
instruction win was modest.

**The parser (`c_parse.c`), -94,60 M.** `c_analyze_semantics` was scanning

**The lexer (`c_source.c`), -30,47 M, and it is back at its floor.** 91,8% of

**The x86 metadata and the arena, -124,22 M, and the per-invocation floor.**

validity stays the form's own cached flag, which `buster_x86_metadata_form()`
checks before any form reaches those facts (**-31,84 M**). And
`arena_allocate_bytes` becomes a `BUSTER_INLINE` bump in `arena.h` with only
the commit outlined: every allocation does the same four operations and the

**Codegen orchestration, the source maps and the object writer, -30,37 M.**

## What this round learned about measuring, which cost it real time

1. **`/tmp` here is a 16 GB RAM-backed tmpfs with a shared per-uid quota, and
   exhausting it does not produce an error you would notice.** It presents as
   a dead shell (commands "produce no output", redirections create empty
   files), as `os_file_write_partially` assertions inside `test_self_host`, as
   spawned test binaries failing with "Exec format error" (730 phantom driver
   failures in one run), as clang's "IO failure on output stream: Disk quota
   exceeded", and as a `cmp: DIFFER` on an innocent patch. Worst, **it silently
   inflates `instructions:u`**, because the compiler's output-write path
   retries: an unmodified baseline binary measured 8.946 M and 9.406 M instead
   of 8.739 M. Two branches lost an A/B to it and one lost a session. **Canary
   the unmodified `ide.before` against the frozen tree before reporting any
   delta**, and `rm -rf build/self-host` after every self-host run.
2. **`cycles:u` is not a usable currency on this host under load**: ±45%
   run-to-run for the *same* binary, against `instructions:u` at 0,002%. Every
   latency and mispredict claim in this audit is therefore stated as a
   structural claim from miss counts, never as a cycles number — including the
   ones where the branch-miss survey made the case for the work.
3. **A fixed-period `instructions:u` sample profile can alias with a hot
   loop.** `c_lex_dispatch` reads **3,68% at period 20.000 and under 2,1% at
   period 200.003** on the same binary and the same workload. Cross-check any
   single symbol's share at two periods before pricing work on it; this one
   sent a branch off with a target 1,8x too large.
4. **Line attribution inside a hot function overstates by roughly 2x from
   skid.** Two lexer items priced at ~11 M from the line profile delivered
   6,1 M; everything priced analytically from a census afterwards held.
5. **Idempotent doubling prices a pass the profile misreads**: run the pass
   twice and take the delta, the downstream state being identical. `perf
   annotate` put the selector's flat LOCAL scan at ~21 M; doubling says
   **12,8 M**. Probes worth keeping as constants of this compiler: a bare
   linked walk of the 1.401.051 IR rows is **10,8 M (7,7 instructions a row)**;
   a bare flat walk of the 1.710.401 machine rows is **4,4 M (2,6 a row)**; the
   opcode-to-exact lookup plus three field reads is **25,7 M (15 a row)**; one
   selector candidate pass is **8,3 M (19 instructions a visit)**.
6. **Symbol-level shares are not comparable across builds.** In a unity build
   the inlining decisions move code between symbols, so a symbol that grew its
   share after a change that provably removed instructions from it has usually
   absorbed a callee. Only whole-program `instructions:u` compares.

## The two items the brief named that were not built, and why

**SPMD codegen over functions with `lane_run` was not built.** Its entire
benefit is wall clock: the previous audit priced it at ~1,3x for codegen alone
and ~2,1-2,8x with function bodies and initializers, for under 10 M
instructions of merge, and showed it is byte-identical by construction because
selection, placement and encoding already run per function in a scratch arena.
None of that is in question. What is in question is whether this round could
have validated it: `cycles:u` on this host under the round's own load repeats
to ±45% for the *same* binary, so the only quantity the change improves could
not have been measured, and shipping a large change to the determinism-critical
part of the compiler on an unmeasurable benefit is not a trade this file should
record as a win. What was done instead is to make its stated prerequisite
checkable rather than described: every per-form and per-coverage cache in the
x86 metadata module fills on the first *serial* touch of its row, and this
round added a third such cache (record validity), so
`buster_x86_metadata_test_unprepared_after_prewarm_all` now calls
`buster_x86_metadata_prewarm_all_forms` and walks the flags themselves —
record validity, normalized row, parsed pattern, coverage validity — asserting
that none is left unfilled. A lane that filled one would race every other lane
reading it, and `BUSTER_CHECK_SERIAL_INITIALIZATION` would only report the
first such fill it happened to reach. The next round on a quiet machine should
take the lever itself.

**The Zen 4 break-even check is still owed, by five kernels now.** The tuning
target in AGENTS.md requires a kernel to justify itself at Zen 4's effective
width; the Ryzen 9 7940HS it names was not reachable from this session, and
neither was a Zen 5 box. The three kernels the previous round left owing this
check — the AVX-512 VBMI base64 decode in `x86_64_metadata.c`, the 64-byte
string-literal decode window in `c_gen.c`, and the identifier mask over the
token shape sidecar in `c_source.c` — are joined by two more: the lex window's
`vpermi2b` class table and the preprocessor's per-64-token class-mask
projection. Both new ones are argued by lane count rather than measured: the
class permute is 64 useful lanes of 64 and each of its four tests likewise, so
the trade is one 512-bit permute plus four 512-bit tests against thirteen
512-bit compares, which is positive on a double-pumped 256-bit datapath by a
wide margin; the class-mask projection costs half a byte per token and repays
itself about 44x. Neither argument substitutes for the measurement.

## Left standing, ranked, priced against the profile of the finished tree

Every share below is against **7.902,2 M**, where 1% = 79,0 M, and carries the
warning from lesson 3: cross-check a symbol at two sampling periods before
pricing work on it.

- **The selector remains the single largest symbol and the largest source of
  both branch and cache misses** — 6,2% of instructions, **17,87% of all
  branch misses**, **14,92% of all L1d misses at 2,01x its cycle share**. This
  round took 9,9 M out of it and established what the rest is: `machine_x86_64.c:6156`
  is the opcode jump table and `:5400` is the exit of a 3,3-iteration inner
  loop whose 262.800 exits match its measured misses — both inherent, with
  switch splitting and peeling already on the do-not-retry list. Line 5290
  could be made branchless for about 1 M extra instructions. The remaining
  structural item is the fourteen per-value side arrays; at 1.161.532 values
  that is the compiler's largest working set and its 2,01x ratio says so.
- **The encoder's capacity pre-pass**: a whole redundant traversal of the
  1.710.401 machine rows costing **16,1 M** by idempotent doubling, of which
  only 4,4 M is the walk itself. A compact `{u16 flat, u8 kind}` budget table
  gets 6-8 M; eliminating the pass needs the row budget accumulated by the
  builder, which is an additive field on `MachineFunction` in a shared header.
- **The three large copies above**, worth ~0 M on `instructions:u` and the
  largest memory-traffic item in the compiler. Needs a quiet machine.
- **The cheap subset of the range expansion engine** — one shared LIFO task
  array per `c_preprocess_expand`, the nesting having been checked to be
  strictly LIFO — ~25-40 M for about a day, provided it is a pure removal of
  work and not a trade (see the two negatives).
- **The lowering's four remaining group-fact walkers**, 4,93 M chained-`else if`
  iterations, ~25 M as a class-byte table; and its two per-body-token capacity
  prepasses, 1,54 M tokens each, which could fuse into one, ~20 M.
- **`c_symbol_intern` is now 4,99% of every branch miss in the compile** (1,11 M),
  the largest frontend source, at c_source.c:2866 and :2952, against ~98 M of
  instructions. 673.785 interns at 1,238 probes each with 87,7% hitting on the
  first probe: the misses are the compare, not the probe count. Worth chasing
  only on a host where the improvement can be measured.
- **The exact-template prewarm is ~44 M of the 91,2 M floor** and ~44 M of
  stage 1, since it runs once per invocation: ~1.689 byte templates each
  emitted twice through the metadata encoder to prove its patch field.
  Publishing a family on first encode would charge a compile only for the
  shapes it uses; a stage-1 self-compile names ~50 of the 126 registry rows, so
  it is worth ~20 M on stage 1 and much more on the floor of a multi-file
  build, against a change to the serial-initialization contract.
- **`#embed` (60-75 M, days) and the full range expansion engine (45-65 M,
  weeks) are both recommended against**, for the reasons measured above. They
  were the top two items of the previous round's ranking; this is the main
  thing that changed.
